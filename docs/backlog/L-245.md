# L-245 Ghost face disappears next to real glass or a beacon from some angles

Kind: Bug / Research, low priority.
Status: Open, not reproduced on purpose yet.

A white stained glass ghost next to real white stained glass or a beacon
shows no face at the shared plane from some angles (maintainer, 0.3.0,
`2900d1dc`). `cullAgainstGhosts` treats a real blended full-support block as
covering the ghost face (`realOpaque`), assuming the real face is drawn. The
game also culls the real block's back face from inside it or at some angles,
and draws a beacon beam similarly, so both faces can be absent. Same family
as L-192/L-193/L-194 (real see-through blocks and ghost faces).

## Next
Reproduce with one ghost and one real glass/beacon block, note the camera
side, then decide whether to keep the ghost face where the real block's face
is not visible from the camera. Record this before changing the face rule.
