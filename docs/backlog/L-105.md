# L-105 Performance: find the real bottleneck before optimizing

Kind: Research **(strong model)**
Status: Open; pure groundwork in place, profiler spike not built (needs the maintainer at the controls)

Find what is actually expensive in Bedrock (native C++ on RenderDragon)
before optimizing anything. Hide particles / weather / overlays are
situational wins, not large frame-time gains. Chosen by the maintainer
2026-10-07.

## Decided
- Lamium does not become a renderer replacement. Prefer bounded, measurable
  substitutions that skip, cache, batch or defer one expensive vanilla path
  while keeping vanilla behavior, capability-gated and fail open.
- No optimization is built until step 1 names a bottleneck. Each candidate
  becomes its own L-item with the maintainer.
- Success: better frame time and 1 % lows in heavy scenes, not a higher
  average in an empty world. One proven large bottleneck beats many guessed
  micro-optimizations.
- Hypotheses to test (sources in PROVENANCE.md): rebuild terrain meshes less
  often or off the critical path; submit many small draws as fewer batches;
  skip entities and block entities that cannot be seen; cache or make
  event-driven repeated per-tick work.

## Known
- Schematic ghost pass (2026-10-14, VALIDATION-LOG): with 3086 ghost
  sections it costs 4-6 ms a frame standing still with nothing rebuilt,
  mostly per-section draws (faces, lines, block actors, entities). Candidate:
  merge sections into fewer meshes.
- Pure part: `src/features/research/FrameProfile.h` reports average, worst
  frame, 1 % low (mean of the slowest hundredth) and per-stage time and
  calls per frame; `tests/FrameProfileTests.cpp`.
- Stage boundaries that link in SDK 26.60.4 (each a timing-only hook calling
  the original unchanged): `GameRenderer::renderCurrentFrame` (whole frame),
  `LevelRenderer::preRenderUpdate` (already hooked by FreeCamera terrain),
  `LevelRenderer::renderLevel`, `LevelRendererPlayer::$queueRenderEntities`,
  `$renderBlockEntities` (already hooked by the ghost pass),
  `$renderEntityEffects`, `RenderChunkCoordinator::tick` (chunk rebuild
  bookkeeping). No linkable entry yet for chunk mesh building, particles or
  UI; `RenderChunkBuilder` and the particle engine are the next headers.

## Next
1. Profiler spike: a build option like the trace options, never on in a
   release; its hooks are new game dependencies (GAME-UPDATES.md). Measure
   per frame in heavy real scenes (village, large farm, storage room, fast
   flight over new terrain): frame time and 1 % lows, and as hooks allow
   terrain drawing, chunk mesh rebuild count/time and thread, entity and
   block-entity drawing, particles, HUD/UI, draw-call counts. Record in
   VALIDATION-LOG.md.
2. Pick at most one or two dominant costs; write a bounded candidate per
   cost (what is skipped/cached/batched/deferred, how vanilla behavior is
   kept, how it fails open).

Areas to look at first: chunk mesh rebuild scheduling (triggers, what runs
on render/main thread, what can be deferred; likely stutter when turning or
moving); draw submission (many small draws or state changes); visibility
(entities and block entities behind walls or underground in farms,
villages, storage); repeated simulation work only if the profile points
there; particles (Hide particles skips drawing, not creation and updates;
not the main route).
