# L-384 HUD: a wider scale range and placement past the screen edge

Kind: Design
Status: Built (2026-11-26), not checked in game

A player wants HUD elements scaled further than today's 50-200 %, both
larger and smaller; for them this is the part that matters. They also want
to push an element past the screen edge while a key is held in the HUD
editor, and a way to bring back an element that has gone almost entirely
off screen. Pushed past the bottom edge, the paper doll shows only its upper
body, which was their first ask.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-23, 2026-11-26)
- The paper doll gets no cut of its own (no box that grows only sideways,
  no upper-body setting): placement past the edge covers it.
- Rotation (asked for at any angle, then called unimportant by the player)
  and putting the hotbar, hearts and hunger into HUD layout are not part of
  this item and not planned.
- Nothing hides while a key is held: that ask was the edge limit above.
- Scale 25-400 %, steps of 5 % (`hudScaleMin`, `hudScaleMax`); below 50 %
  text needs "standard size". Checked in game, back to 50 % if too poor.
- Alt held while dragging or with the arrows: past the edge and no snapping
  at all (Shift already means "times ten" there, Ctrl selects all in fields
  and is the game's sprint; Alt is free and the usual "ignore snapping").
  Without Alt nothing changes. The idle panel says so in a second hint line.
- An element always keeps 8 units of each side on the screen
  (`hudKeepVisible`), so it can be grabbed back; the 3x3 buttons and Reset
  work as before. No new way back.
- No format change: an offset pointing out past its anchored edge means
  "past the edge" and is kept on any screen; an inward one is clamped inside
  as before (`placeAxis`). Saved layouts only ever have inward offsets.

## Next

chat and scoreboard drawn partly off screen, and whether Alt reaches the
editor at all.
