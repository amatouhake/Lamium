# L-384 HUD: scale above 200 %, and a paper doll cut to its upper body

Kind: Design
Status: Requested by a player (Discord, 2026-11-21); not started, no release target

A player asked for HUD elements scaled past today's 200 % and for the
paper doll moved further down so only its upper body shows. As they put
it, another client lets the paper doll's box grow only sideways when it
is scaled. They also asked for rotated HUD elements and for a key that
hides HUD elements while held.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- The scale limit and the paper doll are looked at first. Rotation and
  the hiding key wait for the player's answers to what they are for.

## Known
- HUD layout moves and scales Lamium's twelve elements and, with Game
  HUD placement on, the game's chat, paper doll, scoreboard and boss
  bars; the hotbar, hearts and hunger are not part of it.
- Each game part has a key stepping Standard, Placed and Hidden; most
  Lamium elements have an on/off key. Nothing hides while a key is held.
  The game's F1 hides the whole HUD and the hand.

## Open
- The new upper limit (`hudScaleMax` in `HudElement.h`, now 200).
- How the paper doll is cut: its box taller than the body allows (so it
  can sit lower than the screen's edge would), or a setting that keeps
  only the upper body.

## Next
Asked the player (2026-11-21) which elements rotation and the held key
are for, whether the game's own HUD too, and what F1 does not do for
them. Then agree the two choices above.
