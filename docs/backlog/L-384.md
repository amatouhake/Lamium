# L-384 HUD: a wider scale range and placement past the screen edge

Kind: Design
Status: Requested by a player (Discord, 2026-11-21, answers 2026-11-22); not started, no release target

A player wants HUD elements scaled further than today's 50-200 %, both
larger and smaller; for them this is the part that matters. They also want
to push an element past the screen edge while a key is held in the HUD
editor, and a way to bring back an element that has gone almost entirely
off screen. Pushed past the bottom edge, the paper doll shows only its upper
body, which was their first ask.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-23)
- The paper doll gets no cut of its own (no box that grows only sideways,
  no upper-body setting): placement past the edge covers it.
- Rotation (asked for at any angle, then called unimportant by the player)
  and putting the hotbar, hearts and hunger into HUD layout are not part of
  this item and not planned.
- Nothing hides while a key is held: that ask was the edge limit above.

## Known
- HUD layout moves and scales Lamium's twelve elements and, with Game
  HUD placement on, the game's chat, paper doll, scoreboard and boss bars.
  Scale is 50-200 % (`hudScaleMin`, `hudScaleMax` in `HudElement.h`).
- Placement is an anchor (the nearer edge) plus an offset; dragging snaps
  at the edge, at a 4-unit inset and to the center lines (HUD.md
  "Placement"). The toolbar's 3x3 "snap to" buttons move the selected
  element.
- The moved paper doll was seen up to the screen edge, not cut off
  (L-309); drawn partly off screen it has not been seen.

## Open
- The new limits (both ends).
- Which key lifts the edge limit while dragging, and whether an element may
  leave the screen completely or keeps a part on it.
- How an element that is hard to reach comes back: the 3x3 buttons once it
  can be selected (a list to select from?), a reset, or the editor pulling
  it back on open.
- Whether the stored offset needs anything new for a place past the edge
  (no change to the settings format is wanted).

## Next
Agree the four choices above, then check in game that the paper doll,
chat and scoreboard draw partly off screen.
