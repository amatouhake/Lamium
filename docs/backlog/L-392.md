# L-392 Settings sidebar: what its numbers mean, and categories of uneven size

Kind: Design
Status: Found in game 2026-11-23 (maintainer); waiting for the maintainer's choice

The settings screen's sidebar names each category with a number such as
"0/4" or "19/20". It is how many of the category's features are on, out
of how many it has (`sectionCount` in `SettingsTableView.cpp`: each
feature's main switch, not every switch). Nothing on the screen says so;
the maintainer read it as a guess and found it hard to understand.

The categories (L-340) are also uneven: on build `14e2c83c` the counts
were View 4, HUD 11, World 12, Items 20, Actions 4, General 1,
so one page is very long and others very short.

## Open
- The numbers: drop them, say what they count (a tip, or words such as
  "4 on"), or show something else (only "on" counts, or nothing for
  categories without features).
- The categories: split Items (inventory, recipes, transfer, ...) and
  World, fold General into another, or keep the grouping and make long
  pages easier to scan (group headings exist since L-340).

## Next
The maintainer chooses; a mockup of the sidebar and one long page may
help before deciding.
