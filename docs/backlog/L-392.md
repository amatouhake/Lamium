# L-392 Settings sidebar: what its numbers mean, and categories of uneven size

Kind: Design
Status: The numbers go (decided 2026-11-23); the categories are being looked into, nothing changed

The settings screen's sidebar named each category with a number such as
"0/4": how many of its features are on, out of those with a switch
(`sectionCount` in `SettingsTableView.cpp`). Nothing said so, and
features used by key alone (Zoom, Freelook, FreeCamera, the calculator)
were not counted: View read 0/4 over seven rows, General 0/1 over five.
The categories (L-340) are also of very uneven size.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-23)
- The sidebar shows no numbers.
- The categories are not changed until the material below is discussed.
  The sidebar's top holds the settings' categories, its bottom the
  screens (Hotkeys, Shapes, Waypoints, Schematics, World map, HUD layout:
  DESIGN "Sidebar"); that split stays.

## Known
Features (rows when every row is expanded, counted from the option and
action catalogs, roughly):

| Now (L-340, 2026-11-22) | Features | Expanded |
|---|---|---|
| View: Camera 3, Look 4 | 7 | 41 |
| HUD: Whole HUD 4, Information 4, Hand and status 6 | 14 | 89 (Information 57) |
| World: In the world 5, Map 7, Schematics 11 | 23 | 126 (Schematics 61, Map 50) |
| Items: Item screens 9, Recipes and plan 4, Hands and equipment 8 | 21 | 95 |
| Actions | 7 | 26 |
| General | 5 | 8 |

Before L-340 (`3f1031c9^`) there were seven categories: Camera and look 8
(with Block highlight), HUD and overlays 16 (the HUD lines, chunk
borders, hitboxes, light levels, shapes, automation status), Map 7,
Schematics 11, Inventory 18, Actions 9 (with Tool Protection and Auto
Elytra), General 8 (with toasts, HUD text, book paste). Schematics had a
category of its own beside the pinned Schematics screen. L-340 regrouped
because General was a mixed bag, HUD-wide settings sat away from HUD,
offhand settings were in five places and Inventory had grown to 18.

Of Schematics' 11 rows, 7 are only headings for keys (move, placement,
layers, show, save, screen, repeat); the switches are Schematics,
Schematic menu, Check and Schematic HUD.

## Open
- Whether to split World (Schematics out again, or its key headings
  folded) and Items, and what the small categories (General 5) become.
- Whether long groups (Information's lines, Map) need a shorter look
  rather than another category.
