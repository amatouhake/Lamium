# L-386 Trace an "any" ingredient past its kind (planks to logs)

Kind: Ready (strong model)
Status: Built, not checked in game

A chest takes "planks (any)". With no member chosen in How to make, the
plan stops there and gathers planks (L-370's rule: a group not chosen is
counted as any, nothing traced past it). Most players would rather be
told the logs. Choosing one member (oak planks) traces to its log, but
fixes the wood; there is no "logs, any wood".

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-22)
- By default an "any" ingredient is traced on where every member is made
  from one input in the same amount (each planks from its own logs, four
  a log): the plan makes it from the inputs as a kind of their own
  ("logs etc. (any)"), held inputs of any member counted together. Where
  the members' recipes do not line up so, it stops as today.
- The members need not all line up (maintainer 2026-11-22): the amount
  most members share decides (planks: one log, four planks), and only
  those members' inputs form the kind; bamboo planks (two from a block)
  are left out of it, though held bamboo planks still count as planks.
- Every input of a lined-up member's one-input recipes is in the kind,
  whichever recipe is chosen (logs, wood, stripped logs and wood, stems
  and hyphae): each makes the same planks.
- "Make" on an "any" step makes the member whose input is most carried,
  as placing an "any" ingredient takes the one most carried.
- (Agent, from the table 2026-11-22) A member lines up by the recipe it
  is made by unless the player chooses, so recycling smelt (nuggets from
  tools), ore smelting (coal) and loops (cobblestone from stone) trace
  nothing; with the 1.26.60 table that leaves planks (to logs) and
  wooden slabs (to planks).
- How to make's group lines offer "gather" too (the group gathered as it
  is, as an item's "gather"), beside "any" (traced) and the members.
  Today group lines have no such option, and an item's recipe line does
  not show for a group at all.
