# L-359 Material plan across features, from the game's recipes

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model)
Status: Open; design first: the recipe viewer, then the plan, are being agreed with mockups before more is built. The calculation and a first Materials-tab view exist

One plan for what the player still has to get: the needs of Schematics,
Enchantment Order and later features in one list, traced through crafting,
smelting and the other recipe steps down to what must be gathered, with a
recipe viewer to look recipes up and choose among them. The agreed design
is in [RECIPES.md](../RECIPES.md).

## Decided
- Order (maintainer 2026-11-17): conversation and mockups first, then
  building. First the recipe viewer in the shape players know: an item list
  beside the game's inventory with a search field, and a recipe view opened
  from it. The plan and other derived parts are thought out after that.
  (The Materials tab's view was built before any of this and grew a "make"
  list with no agreed home.)
- Brought forward, without absolute priority over the small and medium work.

## Open
The maintainer's questions of 2026-11-18 with the agent's answers, none
decided:
- The recipe view over the game's window or as a screen of its own: over
  it, the game's screen kept open underneath (a screen of its own would
  close a chest or furnace), covering the window while it shows.
- Crafting recipes always in a 3x3 grid; other stations in fixed layouts of
  Lamium's own slots that echo the game's screens, not the game's art.
- Shapeless recipes: packed from the top left, with a mark beside the arrow.
- Placing a recipe into the grid as the game's recipe book does: a button
  with its place from the start, built later as its own research with
  [L-165](L-165.md) Mass Craft (it acts in the game; the rest only reads).
- One view, not an option: the shaped grid with a line of totals under it;
  lists use the short form (result, ingredients, station).
- Uses: the same view paging through the recipes that use the item, plus a
  short list to scan; stations as icons, their names on hover.
- Click for recipes and right click for uses in Lamium's own lists; keys on
  the hovered item everywhere, the game's slots included (R and U by
  default, rebindable).
- Recipes and uses share one view; any item in it opens its own recipes,
  with a way back.
Also open:
- One plan with a list of targets, or several plans: by the plan's mockup.
- To research before building: a text field over the game's inventory
  screen (the maintainer has seen it work there; L-130 did not reach the
  OreUI recipe book), and whether the recipe book's state can be read.
- From the prior-art survey (kept outside the repository): an amount that
  leaves no spare, a recipe choice for one plan only.
- The agent's provisional choices in the index: other stations rank after
  the furnace; by-products are not credited; recipes that use their own
  product are left out.
