# L-359 Material plan across features, from the game's recipes

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model)
Status: Open; scope conversation under way, the agent's recommendations below wait for the maintainer

One plan for what the player still has to get: the needs of Schematics,
Enchantment Order and later features in one list, traced through crafting,
smelting and the other recipe steps down to what must be gathered, with a
recipe view to look recipes up and choose among them.

## Decided
Maintainer, 2026-11-17:
- Lamium alone: no link to another mod's recipe data or screens, and the
  recipe work is written without opening another project's source
  ([PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md)). Recipes come from the game (L-116).
- Each feature keeps working alone, with its own materials list as now. The
  plan is a shared part the features register their needs into
  ([INTEGRATION.md](../INTEGRATION.md) principle): a feature that is off
  registers nothing, and the plan works with whichever are on.
- What counts as held is a setting. Carried items and the shulker boxes
  among them are in by default and come first (what the Schematic Materials
  tab counts today). The ender chest and placed chests come later.
- Brought forward, without absolute priority over the small and medium work.

## Open
Agent's recommendations, not decided:
- Build order: a recipe index (a pure snapshot rebuilt per world generation,
  no game pointers kept), the shared needs and the plan calculation, the plan
  view, then the recipe view (its grid is a new shared widget).
- How a recipe is chosen, ingredient groups, fuel, spare output and held
  intermediates: in [L-116](L-116.md).
- Kept between sessions: nothing a feature already keeps (its needs are
  recomputed). Only what the player enters: recipe choices, group picks and
  "stop here" marks in one file for all worlds, an entry the current world's
  recipes lack being ignored, not deleted. The first step saves nothing.
- Targets typed in by hand ("3 beacons"): not asked for yet. If wanted, they
  are saved per world under the waypoints' key, and inherit its open
  problem ([L-358](L-358.md)).
- One count of what is held for the plan and the Schematic Materials tab, so
  both show the same numbers; no settings row until a second source exists.
  Enchantment Order keeps counting the inventory only (the anvil needs the
  stacks there), and its materials are enchanted stacks, not item kinds:
  recipes add little to them.
- Where the plan is shown (own screen, a tab, a HUD list).
- Whether [L-165](L-165.md) Mass Craft becomes the plan's executing end.
