# L-359 Material plan across features, from the game's recipes

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model)
Status: Open; design first: the recipe viewer, then the plan, are being agreed with mockups before more is built. The calculation and a first Materials-tab view exist

One plan for what the player still has to get: the needs of Schematics,
Enchantment Order and later features in one list, traced through crafting,
smelting and the other recipe steps down to what must be gathered, with a
recipe viewer to look recipes up and choose among them. The agreed design
is in [RECIPES.md](../RECIPES.md).

## Decided
- Order (maintainer 2026-11-17): conversation and mockups first, then
  building. First the recipe viewer in the shape players know: an item list
  beside the game's inventory with a search field, and a recipe view opened
  from it. The plan and other derived parts are thought out after that.
  (The Materials tab's view was built before any of this and grew a "make"
  list with no agreed home.)
- Brought forward, without absolute priority over the small and medium work.

## Open
- To research before building (step 1): a text field over the game's
  inventory screen (the maintainer has seen it work there; L-130 did not
  reach the OreUI recipe book); whether the recipe book's state can be
  read; whether the game's window stays undrawn under Lamium's scene on
  the inventory and containers as it does on the anvil.
- Whether the same recipe under two station tabs (furnace, blast furnace)
  feels wrong: to see in game.
- The anvil: left out of the screens that carry the lists because of
  Enchantment Order's panels; the maintainer wonders about switching
  between the two there. Agent: decide once the viewer exists.
- How a feature hands its targets over, and whether a switch per feature
  is the control; Schematics first (Enchantment Order's are enchanted stacks).
- The game's own textures for slot, arrow and flame, if the game's look is
  wanted later.
- From the prior-art survey (kept outside the repository): an amount that
  leaves no spare, a recipe choice for one plan only.
- The agent's provisional choices in the index: other stations rank after
  the furnace; by-products are not credited; recipes that use their own
  product are left out.

## Known
- Step 1, from the code (2026-11-18): the anvil panels read the window's
  box from the `root_panel` control each frame and paint before the game's
  tooltip; presses and the wheel over them are cancelled in the mouse
  listener; hovered slots are known through the container controllers'
  hover hooks. Not there: any other screen's name or window control, the
  recipe book's state, Lamium text input while a game screen is on top
  (text reaches only Lamium's own scene), the scene over other screens
  than the anvil, the cost of icons. `--item_screen_probe=y` asks the game
  for all but the text input.
The Materials tab's "Show raw materials" is built with a make list and seen
in game with faults (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-17); cut back in step 6.
