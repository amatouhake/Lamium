# L-359 Material plan across features, from the game's recipes

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model)
Status: Open; the viewer, search, favorites, the wanted items and placing are built behind the Recipes setting and were seen in game; so were the schematic hand-over and the counted places. The look was reviewed, and what was agreed is built and was seen in game (RECIPES.md "The look")

One plan for what the player still has to get: the needs of Schematics,
Enchantment Order and later features in one list, traced through crafting,
smelting and the other recipe steps down to what must be gathered, with a
recipe viewer to look recipes up and choose among them. The agreed design
is in [RECIPES.md](../RECIPES.md).

## Decided
- Order (maintainer 2026-11-17): conversation and mockups first, then
  building. First the recipe viewer in the shape players know: an item list
  beside the game's inventory with a search field, and a recipe view opened
  from it. The plan and other derived parts are thought out after that.
  (The Materials tab's view was built before any of this and grew a "make"
  list with no agreed home.)
- Brought forward, without absolute priority over the small and medium work.
- Maintainer 2026-11-19: a schematic hands its materials over by a switch
  on its Materials tab; the anvil gets no lists for now. Where a recipe
  can be made is shown: since L-376 as a tab per process, its stations
  listed once in the heading.
- Counted places (maintainer 2026-11-19): a store is unmarked by the
  band's button turning into "no store" and from the places tab; one
  where another block stands is dropped when that is seen; the ender
  chest counts from its first sight on; shulker boxes inside a place
  count with their contents; the Materials tab and the Schematic HUD
  take the same count in the same step.
