# L-359 Material plan across features, from the game's recipes

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model)
Status: Open; the recipe index, the plan calculation (checked in game through `recipe_trace`, 2026-11-17) and the service that reads the table are built; no screen asks for it yet

One plan for what the player still has to get: the needs of Schematics,
Enchantment Order and later features in one list, traced through crafting,
smelting and the other recipe steps down to what must be gathered, with a
recipe view to look recipes up and choose among them.

## Decided
Maintainer, 2026-11-17 (build order to the held count on the agent's
recommendation):
- Lamium alone: no link to another mod's recipe data or screens, and the
  recipe work is written without opening another project's source
  ([PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md)). Recipes come from the game (L-116).
- Each feature keeps working alone, with its own materials list as now. The
  plan is a shared part the features register their needs into
  ([INTEGRATION.md](../INTEGRATION.md) principle): a feature that is off
  registers nothing, and the plan works with whichever are on.
- What counts as held is a setting. Carried items and the shulker boxes
  among them are in by default and come first (what the Schematic Materials
  tab counts today). The ender chest and placed chests come later.
- Brought forward, without absolute priority over the small and medium work.
- Build order: a recipe index (a pure snapshot per world generation), the
  shared needs and the plan calculation, the plan view, then the recipe view.
- Recipe choice, groups, fuel, spare output, held items: [L-116](L-116.md).
- Kept between sessions: only what the player enters (recipe choices, group
  picks, "stop here" marks), in one file for all worlds; an entry the
  current world's recipes lack is ignored, not deleted. Nothing at first.
- One count of what is held for the plan and the Schematic Materials tab;
  no settings row until a second source exists. Enchantment Order keeps
  counting the inventory only, and its materials are enchanted stacks, not
  item kinds: recipes add little to them.
- The plan has its own screen and is reachable from the inventory screen.
  It shows first in the Schematic Materials tab, as raw materials and steps
  for what that tab lists as left (the selected placement, its layers
  setting); the plan screen comes after.

## Open
- What the inventory screen shows. The maintainer leans to the recipe view
  being usable there (an item list to look recipes up from), with the plan
  beside it. Agent: a short plan list first, the recipe view joining it at
  its step in the build order. Unchecked: room beside the game's own recipe
  book, and a text field over a vanilla screen (L-130's search path did
  not cover the OreUI recipe book).
- Targets typed in by hand ("3 beacons"): not asked for yet, but "add this
  item to the plan" is the recipe view's natural entry. If wanted, they are
  saved per world under the waypoints' key, and inherit its open problem
  ([L-358](L-358.md)).
- Whether [L-165](L-165.md) Mass Craft becomes the plan's executing end.
- From the prior-art survey (2026-11-17, kept outside the repository), none
  decided: an amount that leaves no spare, a recipe choice for one plan
  only, folding steps, only what can be made now, the steps as a to-do list.

## Known
- `src/features/recipes/RecipeIndex.h` (pure, `tests/RecipeIndexTests.cpp`):
  the table by name, the default recipe per item with L-116's rules, groups
  (a tag, or the inputs that smelt to one item) and `plan()`: wanted, held,
  picked group members and "stop here" items in, what to gather and make
  out. A group is covered by what its members have left after their own
  needs.
- The agent's provisional choices in it, not agreed: items go by name, so
  aux variants are one item (a bed plans as its first color, tipped arrows
  as arrows); blast furnace, smoker, campfires and smithing rank after the
  furnace; by-products (a cake's buckets) are kept but not credited;
  recipes that use their own product are left out.
- `RecipeSnapshot.cpp` fills it from the client's table, 600 recipes a call,
  no game pointer kept. `RecipeService.cpp` runs it on the client tick once
  a screen asks, behind the RecipeTable capability. No screen asks yet.
