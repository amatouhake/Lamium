# L-359 Material plan across features, from the game's recipes

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model)
Status: Open; design first: the recipe display, the plan screen and how they fit together are to be agreed with mockups before more is built (maintainer 2026-11-17). The calculation and a first Materials-tab view exist

One plan for what the player still has to get: the needs of Schematics,
Enchantment Order and later features in one list, traced through crafting,
smelting and the other recipe steps down to what must be gathered, with a
recipe view to look recipes up and choose among them.

## Decided
Maintainer, 2026-11-17:
- Lamium alone: no link to another mod's recipe data or screens, and the
  recipe work is written without opening another project's source
  ([PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md)). Recipes come from the game (L-116).
- Each feature keeps working alone, with its own materials list as now. The
  plan is a shared part the features register their needs into
  ([INTEGRATION.md](../INTEGRATION.md) principle): a feature that is off
  registers nothing, and the plan works with whichever are on.
- Order from here: conversation and mockups first, then building. First
  the recipe viewer in its familiar shape: an item list beside the game's
  inventory screen, always there, with a search field, and a recipe view
  opened from it. The plan and other derived parts are thought out after
  that. (The Materials tab's view was built before any of this and grew a
  "make" list with no agreed home.)
- The list moves aside when the game's own recipe book is switched on.
- Targets can be added to the plan by hand; they are saved per world, under
  the waypoints' key with its open problem ([L-358](L-358.md)).
- The Schematic Materials tab shows what is left as things to gather, no
  more; the steps to make belong to the plan screen.
- A group (any planks) shows its members in turn, the icon changing every
  few seconds; members the player holds come first, then older items; all
  members appear as a grid of icons (a new shared widget), not a row each.
- A step names every station that can do it, as icons.
- What counts as held is a setting. Carried items and the shulker boxes
  among them are in by default; one count for the plan and the Materials
  tab; no settings row until a second source (ender chest, placed chests)
  exists. Enchantment Order keeps counting the inventory only.
- Kept between sessions: only what the player enters (recipe choices, group
  picks, "stop here" marks), in one file for all worlds; an entry the
  current world's recipes lack is ignored, not deleted.
- The plan has its own screen and is reachable from the inventory screen.
- Recipe choice, groups, fuel, spare output, held items: [L-116](L-116.md).
- Brought forward, without absolute priority over the small and medium work.

  OreUI recipe book), and whether the recipe book's state can be read.

  leaves no spare, a recipe choice for one plan only, only what can be made.

  by-products are not credited; recipes using their own product are out.
