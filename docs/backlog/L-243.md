# L-243 Large terrain gaps that only reload when the player comes near

Kind: Bug / Research
Status: Not reproduced; watch item, reopen with the conditions if it returns

Maintainer report 2026-10-22 on `fca2ef9` (0.3.0), local world, after
dimension changes, world map teleports and FreeCamera use: large areas of
terrain, near and far, were missing (sky through the ground). Looking at
them did not bring them back; only walking or flying near reloaded them.
Unlike earlier glitches (distance fog hiding), it did not recover on look.

## Open
If it returns: which change since 0.2.3 is involved. Candidates: the
FreeCamera spectator culler gate and underground hold (L-207/L-213/L-214),
the distance fog change (L-212), map recording centred on the camera
(L-241), or teleports themselves. Whether it happens without FreeCamera,
with map recording off, with Hide effects off, or on 0.2.3.

## Next
On a return: the maintainer's A/B answers, then a bounded trace if needed.
The same-day A/B (everything on; FreeCamera, map recording and Hide effects
each used or avoided) never lost already generated Overworld chunks again.

## Tried
- Not a Lamium cause: in the Nether, never-generated chunks generate only
  out to about the fog distance around the body, whatever the render
  distance; FreeCamera moving alone generates nothing, which with fog
  hidden looks like missing terrain. Generation follows the body (vanilla).
