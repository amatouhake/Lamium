# L-170 Enchantment order planner

Kind: Feature, Experimental (the remaining choices are Design)
Status: Built on `main` after 0.6.0 (not in a release) and checked in game step by step; runtime checks and open choices remain

Given an item and the enchantments wanted, show the order of anvil
combinations that costs the least experience and avoids steps the anvil
refuses as too expensive. Requested 2026-10-12.

Current behavior and the decisions in force: [ENCHANT-ORDER.md](../ENCHANT-ORDER.md)
(read "Current behavior"; its dated record is not needed to pick up work).

## Decided
- Built 2026-11-14, not checked in game: "List the steps beside the anvil"
  is off for a new install and switches all three beside-the-anvil parts (no
  separate main switch: the planner only opens on request). Esc in the
  planner opened from the anvil's "Change" button or by the key closes the
  screen (back to the anvil or the game); its Back button goes to the
  settings table.
- Left out by decision, may come later: planning from the books carried,
  merging two books of one enchantment, a plain book as the item.

## Open
The maintainer chooses:
- A slot item is taken as the plan's item when nothing is chosen and there
  is no plan, beside the decision "not taken from the left slot by itself".
- The plan stays in memory across a world exit (read from the code, not
  checked in game); should it be cleared there?
- A stack carried on the pointer is not marked (left out: it flickered and
  did not fit the held size).
- Not built: dragging a scrollbar and text search in the anvil's choosing
  panel. `enchantOrder.heldUnknown` is translated but unused: a main-hand
  item whose anvil uses cannot be read is planned as fresh with no notice.
