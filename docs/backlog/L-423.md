# L-423 Schematic in-world 3D interaction: touch the placement to change it

Kind: Design / Research (strong model)
Status: Open; idea agreed as its own item (maintainer 2026-11-28)

Working a schematic is complex, and none of today's ways is plainly easy
(SCHEMATIC-CONTROLS.md):
- The schematic screen shows things large but is not intuitive, and how
  a change looks in the world is hard to tell from there.
- The radial menu is close to working in the world and needs no key
  setup, but has many items whose names do not say what happens.
- Hotkeys are quick once learned, but must be learned.

All three ask the player to pick a named item and imagine its effect.
In-world 3D interaction may feel unlike any of them: the player touches
the placement itself (a handle, an arrow, a cutting plane) and sees the
result while doing it, as in a 3D editor. Lamium can move the view
freely (FreeCamera), which helps here.

## Decided
- Its own item, one of the four schematic problems in
  [L-421 Schematic ghost look and aiming by what is drawn](L-421.md)
  (maintainer 2026-11-28): it covers every schematic action, not only
  layers.
- This item's problems: opening the menu costs effort, item names do not
  say what happens, hotkeys must be learned, and switching layers by hand
  is tedious. Switching layers is this item's (which layers are drawn is
  L-421's).
- Hotkeys and the radial menu stay as shortcuts.

## Open
- Which actions gain from direct handling. Candidates: the shown layer as
  a horizontal cutting plane dragged by a handle on the outline; move,
  rotate and mirror by arrows and rings on the placement; look changes
  shown at once.
- Whether the layer follows the player (lowest unfinished layer nearby);
  it risks picking a layer the player did not expect.
- How the player points: the crosshair (aim at a handle, hold use and
  drag; close to the game's own feel) or a free mouse cursor with
  FreeCamera (closer to a CG program).
- What is easy to understand without names, and what still needs a
  label next to the handle.

## Next
Research, then a small trial: the layer cutting plane alone, since
switching layers hurts most today.
- Can a free mouse cursor show over the world without a screen hiding
  it, and keep the world drawn and the view movable?
- Can text be drawn in the world next to a handle (lines and boxes
  already are, src/overlay)?
- Picking a handle is ray-against-box geometry: pure code with tests,
  shared with aiming by the drawn boxes (L-421).
May run alongside L-421 and L-265.
