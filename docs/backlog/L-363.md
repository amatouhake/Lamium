# L-363 The game's tooltip and item details for a listed item

Kind: Research
Status: Open, not started

The item list, the favourites and the recipe view name an item under the
pointer and no more. Items that share a name (potions, firework stars)
cannot be told apart, and Lamium's item details do not show there
(maintainer, 2026-11-18, after the first check of [L-359](L-359.md)).

## Known
- The lists draw after the game's screen; the game's tooltip is drawn by
  the screen for its own slots, from the hovered slot's stack.
- Lamium keeps a stack per listed item for its icon (`SchematicItems`),
  built from the name and aux, without the data a creative entry carries.

## Open
- Whether the game's tooltip can be asked for a stack that is in no slot,
  or whether Lamium draws the item's own text lines itself.
- Whether the listed stack should be the creative entry's own, so a
  potion's effect and a firework star's color come with it.
- How item details ([ITEM-DETAILS.md](../ITEM-DETAILS.md)) hook in.
