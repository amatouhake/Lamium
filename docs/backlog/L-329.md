# L-329 Health a food would give back, beside its food values

Kind: idea, recorded and not pursued (maintainer 2026-11-13)
Status: Parked; not pursued unless the maintainer takes it up

Beside the hunger and saturation a held food restores, show how much health
eating it would bring back.

## Open
A food restores hunger and saturation only; health follows from natural
regeneration, so the number is an estimate (an upper bound that sprinting,
jumping and damage lower), and drawing an estimate is doubtful in itself.
The maintainer recalls Bedrock regenerating more slowly than Java, which
widens the gap between estimate and what is seen.

## Next
If taken up: first measure Bedrock's regeneration rule in game, then decide
whether an upper bound is worth showing at all.
