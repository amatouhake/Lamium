# L-379 A recipe's own controls and crafting onto the pointer

Kind: Design
Status: Done 2026-11-21, checked in game (VALIDATION-LOG); a server unchecked; placing with the pointer full went to L-381

Agreed in [L-371](../L-371.md): a recipe in the recipe view gets its own
controls, and a listed item that can be made can be crafted onto the
pointer.

## Decided
- A control on the recipe chooses it as the item's recipe, there,
  without going to "How to make" (which still lists it).
- No per-recipe "wanted" (maintainer 2026-11-21): the heading's
  "favorite" and "wanted" cover the item, and W over a product slot adds
  that product. This replaces L-371's second control.
- "Place" on every station's layout, at the face's bottom right, shown
  when the open screen is that station's ([L-380](../L-380.md) for cooking;
  stonecutter and smithing still to research).
- A press on the chosen recipe goes back to automatic.
- The choice is a vanilla-style check box (DESIGN's exception for the
  game-grey window); "place" keeps today's picture; the window gets wider
  (to 236, below). Maintainer 2026-11-21, mockup v3.
- "Automatic" has no word: an empty box is unused, a dark check the
  rule's pick, a green check the player's; the tip says which. The box
  is sunk as a slot, without an edge; controls on the game's grey take a
  dark grey edge, not black (maintainer 2026-11-21, after the first build).
- Choosing does not reorder: the order is taken when the item is shown.
- The check box stands left of "place" at the face's bottom right, two
  units apart, both 11 units. Stonecutting too.
- Padding 5 between the content and the face's edge, the window 236
  wide: faces 102 x 64 (crafting), 6 apart, rows 70 (crafting's three
  make the window 12 taller). Maintainer 2026-11-21, mockup v5.
- Ctrl + a press on a listed item that can be made crafts one onto the
  pointer; a plain press and Shift keep placing. A fixed press, listed
  in Hotkeys ([L-372](../L-372.md)).
- Crafting onto the pointer (maintainer 2026-11-21): the recipe in use
  where it fits the grid, else the first that fits, groups as "place"
  picks; what lay in the grid goes back first, and what a recipe leaves
  there after; onto an empty pointer, or one holding the product with
  room (set down in a free slot and picked up with the round). Each press
  one more round. Inventory and crafting table, crafting recipes only.
  Ctrl + "place" in the recipe view crafts that recipe. Seen in game:
  the output take crafts (`250d384d`).
- No press is explained in an item's tip; the place button's tip names
  Shift and Ctrl and why Ctrl would not work now. Shift + press places as
  many as divide evenly in every list, not only "can be made"; a press
  that cannot act shows the recipes (maintainer 2026-11-21).
- Placing and Ctrl with the pointer full: [L-381](../L-381.md).
