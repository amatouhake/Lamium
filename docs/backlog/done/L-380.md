# L-380 Place a recipe at every station

Kind: Feature
Status: Done 2026-11-21, checked in game (VALIDATION-LOG); a server unchecked; brewing waits on L-361

"Place" lays a crafting recipe into the inventory's or the crafting
table's grid. The game's own recipe book places a cooking recipe's input
too (maintainer 2026-11-20, seen while checking [L-376](L-376.md)),
and every recipe the view shows should be placeable where its station is
open (maintainer 2026-11-21).

## Known
- The stations' collections, from the game's UI files (2026-11-21):
  `furnace_ingredient_items` and `furnace_fuel_items` (furnace, blast
  furnace, smoker), `stonecutter_input_items`,
  `smithing_table_template_items`, `smithing_table_input_items`,
  `smithing_table_material_items`; the brewing stand's
  `brewing_input_item`, `brewing_result_items` (3), `brewing_fuel_item`.
- A stack moves straight from a slot to a cell with the manager's
  `handlePlaceAmount` ([L-381](L-381.md)); the same for these slots,
  not seen yet.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- A press lays one in; Shift as much as carried up to a stack (the
  furnaces, the stonecutter) or as many as divide evenly (smithing: the
  template, the base and the addition, one of each a round).
- A slot holding another item is emptied into the inventory first (a
  furnace stops smelting it); one holding the same item gets more.
- Fuel is never touched, as the game's book does not choose it.
- The stonecutter gets its input only for now; the player picks the
  result in its list.
- "Place" shows on the tab of the open screen's station: smelting at the
  furnace, blasting at the blast furnace, smoking at the smoker,
  stonecutting, smithing.
- At a station "can be made" lists what that station makes from what is
  carried, and a press places there.
- Ctrl + press (crafting onto the pointer) stays with crafting recipes.
- Brewing waits on [L-361](L-361.md): the recipe table has no brewing.

## Decided later (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- The stonecutter's result: Lamium cannot choose it (tried,
  VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-21: the choice is the screen's own state, the
  screen's slot press on its list `stones` chose nothing, the other calls
  do not link). Instead, after Lamium lays the input in, a game-style
  tip over the result list asks for a result until the list is pressed,
  the input empties or changes, or the screen closes (A in
  [demos/stonecutter-notice.html](../../demos/stonecutter-notice.html)).
  It stands above the list's panel: the list starts at the panel's top.
- Shown at a station, an item's recipes open on that station's tab
  where it has one.
