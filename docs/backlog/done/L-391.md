# L-391 The plan's "Gather": liquids, and natural stones it would craft

Kind: Ready
Status: Done 2026-11-23, checked in game (VALIDATION-LOG)

Two things the plan said that a player would not do, seen with a
schematic's remainder among the wanted items (build `14e2c83c`): water
listed under "Gather", counted as the schematic counts liquids (one per
source cell), and granite, diorite and andesite made by default by their
crafting recipes, which take Nether quartz.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-23)
- Water and lava are not handed to the plan: the plan is about items to
  gather and make, and a bucket count would not fit (water spreads from
  two sources). They stay in the schematic's Materials tab as they are
  (`planTargets`, `Verification.h`).
- Granite, diorite and andesite are usually gathered, named as leather
  and white wool are (a recipe turns something rarer, Nether quartz, into
  them): "automatic" gathers them, choosing a recipe still makes them,
  and their polished forms are still made from them (`usuallyGathered`).
- Natural blocks are not gathered by default as a rule: only what most
  players would gather rather than make is named.
- Wheat reads "Automatic" with no input or station: it is the base of
  its loop with the hay bale, so it is gathered. Not a fault.
