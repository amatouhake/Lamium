# L-330 Radar: mob icons that draw wrongly, then icons by default

Kind: Design
Status: Done 2026-11-26; faces rebuilt from the turned model, per look, faces by default, all seen in game (last `ec0af34c`)

Some mobs' icons on the radar did not draw correctly (maintainer
2026-11-13), so the radar kept its Experimental badge.

## Decided
- The radar draws faces by default (`map.radarFaces`, maintainer
  2026-11-26, after the rebuilt faces were seen in game); saved settings
  keep their value.
- How a face is seen (maintainer 2026-11-26, `faceRecipe` in
  `features/map/MapFaces.h`): the front of the "head" with the parts
  hanging from it, turned as the model is; tack, spikes and parts the game
  hides left out; a kind without a head shows the part where its face is
  (squid's body, the slime with its eyes on top, wither's middle head, the
  shulker's shell, the snow golem's carved pumpkin unless sheared); long
  necks cut (llamas); camels, turtles, fish and parrots in profile;
  horses, donkeys, mules and armadillos from an angle; the frog whole;
  armor stands stay dots.
- A face per look: the texture the mob's variant picks for horses, cats,
  llamas, parrots, rabbits, axolotls, foxes, frogs, wolves, mooshrooms,
  pandas and shulkers; the elder guardian's by its flag.
- One texel size for every mob; faces past 15 texels are halved by
  averaging (squid, ghast, strider, guardians).

## Known
- The `radar_icon_probe` sweep writes every renderer's face once per world
  to logs/radar-sweep.*. The game's model files
  (`data/resource_packs/vanilla*/__brarchive`, a flat archive of JSON and
  PNG) match what the renderers hold: a part's pivot is its offset from its
  parent's with y the other way, a root's from y 24; rotations keep the
  file's signs in radians. The render controllers there list each kind's
  textures by variant.
- Left as they are: the dragon's eyes are on its head's sides, so its face
  reads as a profile; a halved guardian's red eye mixes toward orange;
  horses are a little harder to tell than donkeys.
- Not seen: a server, other resource packs' models.
- The Experimental badge on the radar comes off only as BACKLOG.md's badge
  rule says, by the maintainer.
