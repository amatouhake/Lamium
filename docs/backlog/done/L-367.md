# L-367 Game-like controls on the recipe windows; wanted items down to 0 by the wheel

Kind: Design
Status: Done 2026-11-20: the recipe view and the controls seen in game; the plan window's rows built and seen, the plan itself rethought in L-370; open fixes in L-369

With the recipe view and the plan window drawn as the game's window
(L-366), Lamium's dark tabs, buttons and switches on them stood out: an
off button or switch was a dark block on the light window (maintainer
2026-11-19, from screenshots). The wanted stepper's minus took an item out
at one, but the wheel and the wanted key's wheel stopped at one.

## Decided
- Second look (maintainer 2026-11-20, after seeing the first): the
  windows' tabs are the game's, outside the windows on their top edge
  (the window starts 19 units down, its heading one line: name, pager,
  the two buttons; the plan window has no title); unchosen tabs in the
  slots' grey; pictures 16 units in the windows, 14 on the band, whose
  tabs are 14 high (one row of the band's list fewer at 360 high) over a
  one-unit rule. Favourite and wanted stay Lamium's joined buttons, green
  when on, with the switch's lighter off face (to see whether that
  settles them before trying the game's button). No sound for now. Row
  buttons stay raised.
- The wheel takes a wanted item to 0, shown faint; it goes out once the
  pointer leaves its row or the wanted key is up (RECIPES.md); seen and
  settled 2026-11-20.

- The plan window (maintainer 2026-11-20, after a comparison of what
  recipe viewers offer, kept outside the repository): it stays tabs of
  lists, no tree. Above a list, one line of the whole: what is left of all
  there is to gather (count and percent) with a bar; each row has have /
  need and a short bar. A row of what to make shows its inputs with
  counts, an arrow, its product with the count made, the crafts and the
  station, in the order to make them. Rows that are done (gathered, or
  made enough) turn faint and move under the others.
- Choosing for the plan (maintainer 2026-11-20): a row of what to make
  with several recipes steps through them in place (previous / next and
  "1 / 3"), its inputs changing at once; a row with one recipe has no
  such control. An input any of several items serves is picked there too,
  by a press on its icon. The recipe view is for looking things up: it
  has no "use" button and no "in use" word; while the item is in the plan
  the recipe in use carries a small mark by its product, named under the
  pointer.
- The recipe view (maintainer 2026-11-20): two columns, three rows, six
  recipes a page, with room between them and inside the window's edge.
  A two-line heading: the item's name across the width with favourite and
  wanted at its right end; under it the station's name and the pager,
  previous / "1 / 3" / next. "Place" is a small square button with a
  picture drawn by Lamium at the recipe's upper right, named under the
  pointer, faint when it cannot place, with the reason under the pointer.
  Page buttons also beside the item list's "1 / 9"; the wheel still turns
  pages everywhere. After seeing it (2026-11-20): the heading's name has
  the item's icon before it and its tooltip under the pointer; the pager
  keeps the width of two-digit pages; raised buttons have half-unit light
  and shadow, a disabled one is flat and darker; the place picture stays
  an arrow; tips over the light windows are the game's tooltip box. The
  recipe book's width is L-368. The pager's slash keeps its place
  (page right-aligned before it, count after it). Under "can be made" a
  press places the item's recipe (RECIPES.md). Third round: the tabs as
  measured from the game; the window three rows high in the middle; the
  pager's slash with equal room; "can be made" from the inventory itself
  and the grid's size; a recipe already laid is left alone. Fourth: the
  tabs take the window's stepped corners; Shift leaves a grid that holds
  as many as divide; the heading keeps 7 units from the window's edge.
  Fifth: a missing slot reddens only its face; a press on a laid recipe
  adds one more of each.
