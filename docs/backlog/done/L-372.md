# L-372 Hotkeys: where and on what each action works

Kind: Ready
Status: Done 2026-11-27; every choice built and seen in game (`384c3626`)

A binding used to conflict with another when their chords overlapped and
both were gameplay actions or both item-screen actions; every other case
was written into the code. The recipe screens add places (the recipe
view, the plan window, the bands' editor, the search field) and presses
on Lamium's own parts, so the rule says where an action works and on what.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20; the layer draft 2026-11-22)
- Lamium-wide, for every action: each declares where it works and, where
  it matters, on what. Two bindings conflict only where both meet; the
  conflict tip says where. The code's special cases become declarations.
- The layers: gameplay, an item screen, a Lamium window over it (recipe
  view, plan window, bands' editor: one layer) and outside a world.
  Typing and Lamium's own screens (settings, world map, Schematics) are
  no layer; there they keep their own fixed keys. A window lets through
  only the actions declared for it (R, U, A, W, back, Ctrl+F), not the
  item screen's others (Sort, Mass Craft, Transfer).
- Presses on Lamium's own parts (left: recipes, right: uses, Shift and
  Ctrl with them, the wheel) stay fixed, not bindings, and are listed:
  Hotkeys shows them as read-only rows, like Item transfer's gestures.
- Settings ids and saved bindings do not change.
- Hotkeys (maintainer 2026-11-23, demos/hotkey-layers.html B): a place
  column before the key column, short names (Play, Screen, Window,
  Outside); the footer names the places in full, on what and the
  condition.
- One tree for both screens (maintainer 2026-11-23). Read-only keys
  (faint unfilled caps, a hover tip when cut) on the settings screen too:
  Item transfer's gesture rows are named by their effect, keep their
  switch and show their keys read-only; the recipe lists' presses are
  read-only child rows of Recipes. The place column stays in Hotkeys.
- Hotkeys draws the settings tree (group, feature, child) always open,
  without chevrons: the feature row carries its toggle key, children are
  child actions, keyed options (by the option's name) and fixed presses,
  in grey like the settings' child rows. Search and Find by key show a
  hit child under its parent without its siblings; a feature-name hit
  shows all its children; pressing a parent while searching shows them.
- A name shown alone (conflict tip, footer, capture line, toast) is
  composed like the toast: "Feature > child" with an ASCII " > ", the
  feature alone for its toggle key. Names that exist only to stand
  alone (toggle and keyed-option actions) are removed.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-27)
- Built 2026-11-22/23 and kept as seen: `input::whereActs` decides what
  fires and what conflicts; `input::fixedPresses` lists the fixed presses.
  Conditions never keep bindings apart; the tip ends each listed binding
  with where it meets, in faint text; the full place names and the fixed
  rows' names stay as built.
- No meanings by target: one meaning per key per layer.
- Esc and the game's inventory key: the order already built (typing, the
  bands' editor, the recipe view, the plan window, the game's screen).
- A warning where a binding takes a key the game uses where the action
  works: on item screens (inventory, drop, hotbar numbers; seen in game
  2026-11-27) and in play (every key of the game's keyboard controls,
  maintainer 2026-11-27), as SETTINGS-KEYMAP.md "Where actions work" says:
  with the game's own modifiers, filled caps, the tip's list toned.
