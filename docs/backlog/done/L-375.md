# L-375 The band layout editor

Kind: Ready (strong model)
Status: Done 2026-11-20, checked in game (VALIDATION-LOG)

Let the player arrange the item screens' bands, as agreed in
[L-371](L-371.md) "Decided: the bands' arrangement" from the third
mockup ([demos/band-layout.html](../../demos/band-layout.html), tabs off).

## Decided
- Opened over the inventory from a "Layout of the bands" link row on
  Recipes (the item list and the favourites) and on the Plan; disabled
  outside a world ([L-373](L-373.md)). The link closes the settings
  and opens the inventory (`LocalPlayer::openInventory`); Esc or the
  inventory key ends the editor first.
- Dragging only: a panel is grabbed anywhere; the layout after the drop
  shows while dragging; over another panel of its region it swaps, in
  another region it goes before or after the one it is over, into an
  empty region it fills it. Over its own place nothing is said; a place
  that takes nothing says so. A tray holds pages not placed.
- Lines between panels size them in 5 % steps, never under one row.
- Reset layout (two presses) and Done (Esc), as in the HUD layout editor.
- While editing: empty regions dotted, a region lent to a side on this
  screen marked.
- Taking a page out only hides it; its feature works by its setting.

## Known
- The game's tabs stand on the window's top on the inventory, the crafting
  table and the furnace; the region above starts over them, measured from
  the screen's controls (`band::windowTop`).

## Next
Nothing; the narrower screen widths are seen in passing.
