# L-378 Light up the game's slots that match the search

Kind: Research
Status: Done 2026-11-21, checked in game (VALIDATION-LOG); a hitch on closing a screen now and then not told apart from the world loading

While the search field holds text, the slots of the open game window
whose items match are lit up, so a chest's contents can be searched
(agreed in [L-371](../L-371.md); RECIPES.md keeps the field's place for it).

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21, [demos/search-bar.html](../../demos/search-bar.html))
- Switched by a square button beside the search field (its height, the
  first of its buttons), green when on, kept; off by default. Never by an
  unseen gesture such as a double press on the field.
- While it is on and the field holds text, the slots that do not match
  are darkened. If darkening proves hard to draw over the game's slots, a
  frame on the matches is the fallback (to be agreed again).
- The button's picture: a 3x3 grid of cells with only the middle one light
  (candidate B).

## Known
- The slots are found among the screen's controls: a small square one
  (the `*_grid_item`, 18 units) whose collection
  `UIControl::findAllCollectionIndices` names, its cell from
  `findCollectionIndex` with that name (the index the first hands its
  callback is garbage; slot_trace, VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-21), kept where
  the screen's manager knows that index. The collection components'
  vtables are not exported, so they cannot be told apart directly. The log
  names how many slots the first screen had.
- Only where items are kept (collections named inventory, hotbar or
  container): darkened crafting cells read as broken (maintainer
  2026-11-21).
- Found once the window rests, not every half second; nothing drawn while
  it slides (the cells were left behind by the opening animation); a slot
  matched again only on a change: the walk every half second and matching
  every frame made the game heavy (maintainer 2026-11-21).
- The darkening is drawn after the game's screen and before Lamium's bands;
  the game's tooltip is held back and drawn after, as for the bands.
