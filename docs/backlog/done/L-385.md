# L-385 Make a plan's steps from its "to make" tab

Kind: Ready (strong model)
Status: Done 2026-11-23, checked in game on a local world and a local BDS (VALIDATION-LOG); a round accepted in part on a real server not seen

"To make" lists the plan's steps with their station, what can be made now
and what is needed. Making them still means opening each recipe. The
plan knows the recipe (How to make), the members and the count, so a
step can be laid in, or made, from its row.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- Both laying in and making are offered, each in one action (no second
  press to confirm making); making goes through an explicit control,
  never a bare press on a row. The look is agreed from a mockup.
- The panel's "to make" rows offer them too: most making will start
  there, not in the window. The panel is settled first.
- A grid holds one recipe at a time: the heading's "all" only makes.
- Laying in keeps the recipe view's own "place" picture (the same act);
  making, which means more, gets a picture of its own. Both the panel's
  and the window's rows show the two picture buttons under the pointer.
- The stores' common mark is the chest drawn on a container's store
  button.
- Making more than one round sends requests as Mass Craft does, so a
  server check comes before the release that carries this.
- A row makes its step, as much as is still needed. "Make all" (on the
  first heading) makes every step of the open grid in the plan's order,
  each as far as what is carried allows when its turn comes, in part
  too, so a step's product feeds the next; a step that cannot be made is
  passed (2026-11-22: only steps whole at the press left most undone).
  Short of inputs, steps above may use them before the end product. A
  step lays no more rounds than the inventory has room for (empty slots,
  slots the laid inputs empty, room on the product's stacks), and one
  with no room is passed;
  nothing made is thrown away. Only
  steps of the open station (a 2x2 recipe also in the inventory); others
  cannot be pressed and their tip names the station.
- "Can make now" counts what is carried only. Steps carried items fall
  short of but the stores would cover stand under "make after taking
  out", between "made in part" and "short"; an input held only in a store
  has a small chest on its slot (red stays for what nobody holds), and
  its tip lists the stores and counts, as "to gather"'s star does.
- After making, the plan works itself out again: made steps go to held,
  the next rise into "can make now".
- The panel has "Make all" too (2026-11-22): the make picture on the
  line of column headings, over the rows' make buttons, its tip saying
  what it does; greyed where nothing can be made at the open screen.

## Decided: controls and marks (maintainer 2026-11-22, demos/plan-make.html)
- "Lay in" takes the recipe view's presses: one round, Shift what the
  step still needs (as much as the grid takes), Ctrl one round made onto
  the pointer. "Make" makes what is still needed, with no modifier. The
  hint line names them.
- The making picture: a grid turning into one thing (four cells, an
  arrow, a box).
- Under the pointer the count stays; the two buttons come in between the
  name and it.
- The store mark stands before the count, so counts keep their column;
  one mark whether part or all comes from the stores, the tip giving
  carried and each store's count.

## Next
Nothing. A round accepted in part ("make" takes the output without
Mass Craft's check that it is the step's product) cannot be brought
about on a local server; it is in the pre-release checks with L-390 1-7.

## Known
- "Make" is a run of its own in `RecipePlace.cpp` (`place::make`), not
  Mass Craft's (which throws its product out): lay in what is still
  needed (a stack a cell), take the output as a Shift click does, again.
  At a station it lays the rounds in only.
