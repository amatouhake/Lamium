# L-383 Schematic Materials: drop the raw view, call it "材料"

Kind: Ready
Status: Done; seen in game 2026-11-21

The Materials tab was built before the plan could trace recipes. Its
"Show as raw materials" switch gives a raw list with little to adjust,
while the plan (L-359) traces the same recipes with choices, stores and
gathering. The Japanese "資材" also reads less plainly than "材料".

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- The Materials tab keeps what is still to place, as blocks; the raw
  view and its switch go. Raw materials are the plan's: "Put what is
  left into the plan" hands the list over.
- Japanese "資材" becomes "材料" everywhere it names the schematic's
  materials: the tab, the HUD, settings and help. English stays
  "Materials"; the Chinese files keep their words unless they read the
  same way.

## Next
Nothing; closes here. The raw view's session switch was never saved, so
no setting key is involved. The RecipeTable capability now reports under
Recipes. ResourceCalculator's button stays (an outside tool, linked
only).
