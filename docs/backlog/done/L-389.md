# L-389 How to make: one way to offer an item's and a group's choices

Kind: Ready (strong model)
Status: Done 2026-11-22, checked in game (VALIDATION-LOG); the wheel's order not seen

How to make's lines opened their choices in two shapes, and the
review of 2026-11-22 listed what read badly: a group's "gather" a row
of its own for some groups only; "any" a cell among the members, with
a member's picture; "any" meaning made for planks and gathered for
sand; recipe lists very long and in the table's order (re-dyeing before
the recipe from leather, glass and wool); recipes named by their first
input alone, cut and repeated. The agreed shape answers them.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-22)
- Words stay one line, right after each row's pictures (no column),
  with no heading or hint words. Rows have no tip of their own; each
  picture (slot, station) names itself.
- A group's line opens: "<kind>から作る" (made from its kind) where the
  plan traces it (L-386), "<group>を集める" always, each one line with
  its pictures ("*" kept); these replace today's "any" cell. Under them
  the members in a grid from the start, a press deciding one. The words
  come from names alone (planks: "原木から作る", wooden slabs: "板材から
  作る"). A member decided has its own ways as its line under "レシピ",
  as any item; the group's line does not repeat them.
- A group's members stand in the item list's creative order (oak,
  spruce, birch, ..., bamboo, crimson, warped), in How to make, the
  group's tip and the wheel; today it is the game's tag order.
- An item's line opens: "自動" first as today (the rule's recipe, or the
  item where the rule gathers it, faint; the word alone), framed green
  while chosen; it stays a choice of its own, apart from choosing the
  recipe it uses now, since the rule may change. Then "集める", then
  the recipes: the rule's first, the rest in the rule's order (station,
  then the game's priority).
- A recipe row is its slots and station, no words.
- Re-dyeing recipes (another color of the same thing) fold into one
  row, "染め直し（N）", that opens; the chosen one opens it. Its rows
  stand in line with the other recipes (no indent).
- The word stays "集める", as the "集める物" tab says it.
