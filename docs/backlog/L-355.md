# L-355 Color tokens for the schematic check's wrong state and not placed

Kind: Ready (small)
Status: Built in code; the DLL was not built where it was written and nothing was seen in game

`mismatchColor` in `SchematicsView.cpp` colored two of the Check list's
kind squares with color literals, which rule 7 (AGENTS.md) forbids, and
`Check-UiTokens.ps1` did not see them: its pattern looked for `Rgb{`.

## Decided
Maintainer 2026-11-16:
- Two new tokens with the list's values: `palette::wrongState`
  (`1, .8, .25`, yellow) and `palette::notPlaced` (`.75, .85, .9`, pale
  blue), beside `palette::mismatch`. `warning` and `info` keep their own
  meanings.
- The mistake marks in the world (`GhostMesh.cpp`) share the tokens: a
  wrong block or an extra is `mismatch` (was `1, .25, .2`), a wrong state
  `wrongState` (was `1, .8, .2`).
- `Check-UiTokens.ps1` also counts a returned brace list of three numbers
  with a fraction. The eight it found (the shapes' four colors in
  `WorldOverlay.cpp` and `BlockHighlight.h`) now use the `shape*` tokens,
  same values, so the baseline is unchanged.

## Known
- `GhostMesh.cpp` still passes two colors as plain numbers the check does
  not see: the unknown block name's orange outline and the ghost's cyan
  outline. They are not check kinds and have no token.
