# L-194 Save corner marks over real glass or water change with the camera

Kind: Bug / Research, low priority
Status: Parked 2026-10-15 after three tries; resume with one of the ideas in Next

A save corner mark (red or blue) in or against a real see-through block
(glass, water) looks different from either side of a plane that lies on
Chunk Borders lines: from one side the real water behind the mark is hidden,
from the other it shows through. Not over air or opaque blocks; the same in
Fancy and Simple graphics. Maintainer report 2026-10-14/15.

## Decided
- Kept: only the seen sides are drawn, and corner 1 is pulled nearer than
  corner 2 (their shared edge lines no longer trade places).

## Next
Find where the game draws translucent terrain relative to the camera's
chunk, or draw the faces with a material that does not write depth.

## Tried
- Drawing such marks in the block entity alpha pass: no effect.
- Drawing them in the name tag pass: no effect.
- Lifting the faces 0.02 out of the cell: no effect, and looked lifted over
  stone.
