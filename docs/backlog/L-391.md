# L-391 The plan's "Gather": liquids, and natural stones it would craft

Kind: Design
Status: Found in game 2026-11-23 (maintainer); waiting for the maintainer's choice

Two things the plan says that a player would not do, seen with a
schematic's remainder among the wanted items (build `14e2c83c`):

- Water is listed under "Gather" (and on the panel), counted as the
  schematic counts liquids: one per source cell (SCHEMATIC.md
  "Materials"). Water is not an item; the recipe table does not know it,
  so it lands among the wanted items no recipe knows, gathered as they
  are (PLAN.md). Lava would show the same way.
- Granite, diorite and andesite read "Automatic" and are made by their
  crafting recipes (diorite and quartz, cobblestone and quartz, ...),
  so the plan asks for quartz and cobblestone, though players mine these
  stones. Clay (from four clay balls) and other natural blocks with a
  recipe may do the same. The rule for "usually gathered" today is
  `usuallyGathered` (`RecipeIndex.h`: smelted from an ore that drops it,
  leather, white wool), and nothing covers a natural block a recipe also
  makes.

## Open
- Liquids: leave them out of what the schematic hands over, show them as
  buckets (how many: one per source, or a few, since water spreads), or
  keep them with a note that they are placed, not gathered.
- Natural blocks: which count as usually gathered. A named list (granite,
  diorite, andesite, clay, ...), or a rule (a block that generates in the
  world and is mined as itself), and whether the player's own choice of
  a recipe still wins as now.
- Seen in the same screenshot, to ask: wheat's line in "How to make"
  shows no input and no station beside "Automatic".

## Next
The maintainer answers the two choices; then this becomes Ready.
