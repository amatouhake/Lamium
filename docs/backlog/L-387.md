# L-387 One way of writing counts in the recipe tips

Kind: Design
Status: Open (maintainer 2026-11-22: later, after the panel)

The Notes window's and the panel's tips write the same counts in
different ways, and the player cannot tell what a number is. Seen in
game 2026-11-22 (build `05febfa2`):

- A step on "to make": "今作れる: 56  必要: 11,214", then each input
  "鉄鉱石など: 56/11,214  あと 11,158" (window and panel alike now).
- A "to gather" row (`gatherTip`): "所持: 146  必要: 44845" without
  thousands apart, and what is short as "あと 25 チェスト + 23 スタック
  + 27", another meaning of "あと" than the step's.
- A wanted row on the panel: "所持: x  必要: y"; in the window the row's
  tip has the name only (the columns show the counts).
- An input slot: the name, and "必要: 44,844" only from a thousand up
  (the slot shows "44k"); a product slot in a breakdown or How to make:
  inputs as "石: 44,844".
- The window leaves out what its columns show; the panel says it in the
  tip, though it now has the same columns (build `2eb5e456`).
- A group's tip on the panel (a "to gather" row of a kind) is drawn in
  Lamium's grey tip box while every other item tip there is the game's
  tooltip box (`ItemList.cpp`: `game` leaves out `late.tipGroup` unless
  the tip is over the window).

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-22)
- An input is written name first: "石: 146/44,844" (held/needed) where
  what is held is counted, "石: 44,844" where it is not.
- A count cut to fit shows whole in its tip, thousands apart (DESIGN).

## Open
- One form for "held / needed / short" across steps, gathered items and
  wanted rows; whether "short" is in items or in chests and stacks, and
  where each goes.
- Whether the window's tips repeat its columns, so a tip reads the same
  wherever it opens.
