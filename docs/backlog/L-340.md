# L-340 Settings grouping

Kind: Design.
Status: Open, not started.

General is a mixed bag; HUD-wide settings (Toggle toasts, HUD text) sit away
from the HUD section; hiding the paper doll is reachable from three places
and offhand settings from five (maintainer 2026-11-14, noticed before the
review and confirmed by it). Output: the section list and each feature's
place.

## Open
- Whether a screen that only opens on request (the world map, the
  enchantment order planner) needs an on/off switch of its own.

## Decided
- Moving a feature between sections is one table (`featureSection` in
  `SettingsRows.h`) and does not touch saved settings.
