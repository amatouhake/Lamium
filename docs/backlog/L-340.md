# L-340 Settings grouping

Kind: Design.
Status: Open; an agent's draft below (2026-11-17) waits for the maintainer.

General is a mixed bag; HUD-wide settings (Toggle toasts, HUD text) sit away
from the HUD section; hiding the paper doll is reachable from three places
and offhand settings from five (maintainer 2026-11-14, noticed before the
review and confirmed by it). Output: the section list and each feature's
place.

## Decided
- Moving a feature between sections is one table (`featureSection` in
  `SettingsRows.h`) and does not touch saved settings.

## Open
- Whether a screen that only opens on request (the world map, the
  enchantment order planner) needs an on/off switch of its own.
- The draft below, section by section.

## Draft (agent, not decided)
Today: Camera & view, Inventory, Actions, HUD & overlays, General, Map,
Schematics. Proposed (moves in **bold**):
- **General**: Settings key, Game language, Saved data, Skip server packs,
  Calculator. (**Book paste** to Inventory; **Toggle toasts**, **HUD text**
  to HUD.)
- **HUD** (was HUD & overlays): HUD-wide rows first (**HUD text**,
  **Toggle toasts**, Automation status), then Info HUD, Target info, Player
  list, Durability, Inventory HUD, Used slots, Status effects, Offhand slot,
  Saturation, Game HUD (its parts, the paper doll among them), Debug view.
- **In the world** (new, the in-world aids now split between two
  sections): Chunk borders, Hitboxes, Light overlay, Shapes, **Block
  highlight** (from Camera & view).
- Camera & view: Zoom, Freelook, FreeCamera, Night vision, Hide offhand,
  Hide effects, Connected textures.
- Inventory: as now plus **Book paste**; Fake offhand, Offhand swap and Hand
  restock (its offhand option) next to each other.
- Actions, Map, Schematics: unchanged.

## Known
- The paper doll: Game HUD's part row (placed / shown / hidden) and the HUD
  layout editor's tag switch write the same `showPaperDoll` (L-334); the
  third place the maintainer meant was not identified in code.
- Offhand: Hide offhand (Camera & view), Fake offhand, Offhand swap, Hand
  restock's offhand (Inventory), Offhand slot and Durability's offhand
  (HUD). Each is a different feature; the draft keeps each with its kind
  and puts the Inventory ones side by side. A cross-link row would be a new
  control (a Design question).
- `featureSection` falls back to HUD & overlays for any id it misses
  (L-342); a new section needs its translation in four languages.
