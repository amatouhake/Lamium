# L-115 Entity ghosts drawn as models

Kind: Research (strong model)
Status: A compromise version shipped in 0.1.8; the limits below are open

Draw missing schematic entities as their models with the light-blue outline,
not translucent, without a live entity. The shipped version
(`EntityModels.cpp`) is a compromise (maintainer 2026-10-10): many entities
draw light-blue faces instead of their skin, and details such as cushion
colors are missing.

## Decided
How it works now; keep these rules:
- Model: `ActorRenderDispatcher::getDataDrivenRenderer(id)` by identifier
  gives the model and default skin, drawn with `mEntityAlphatestMaterial`.
  Draw geometry "default", else the first that is not baby/charged.
  Entities without a model keep the dashed frame and the world name tags
  (logged once per identifier). At most 64 models a frame.
- Placement: `compileCubes` (untransformed, once per part) emits each part's
  quads relative to its bone pivot (`BoneOrientation::mPivot`, absolute),
  y flipped, cube rotations and inflation applied. Parts are chained in the
  stored y-up space, each turned about its bone pivot; geometry x is
  mirrored in the world. In that space x and z rotations turn the other
  way, y as is. Outlines follow the compiled quads. Do not use `translateTo`
  or `ModelPart::mRot`: the game writes a live entity's pose into the shared
  model (posing one armor stand moved every ghost).
- Pose: bone rest rotations plus the constant parts of the entity's own
  `animation.<name>.*` setup/general animations (rotations and position
  offsets), only from animations whose bones are all in the model. Molang
  only when it needs nothing from the entity (`RestPose.h`, tested):
  numbers, `this` as the bone's rest value, arithmetic. Legacy `.v1.0`
  copies are skipped when the current one exists. A bone the entity's own
  animations leave alone takes a rotation that two or more other entities'
  setup/general animations agree on, when all their bones are in the model
  (the witch's crossed arms come from the villager's).
- Skin: when the default skin is a layer (armor, decor, markings,
  profession, `_none`, baby, saddle, overlay) the model draws light-blue
  faces with the overlay face material (`overlay/FaceMaterial.h`, per
  graphics mode): horses, donkeys, mules, llamas, trader llamas, villagers,
  rabbits.
- The saved `Rotation` yaw turns with the placement (`toWorldYaw`, tested).
- Trace: `xmake f --schematic_model_trace=y` logs each model's geometries,
  skin, animations, pose and per-part pivots once.

## Open
Known limits:
- Poses that need the entity stay at rest: a wolf's tail hangs into the
  body (`query.tail_angle`); zombie-like arms hang down, so a drowned's
  sleeves flicker against its jacket; sitting, walking.
- Layered skins beyond the default: a sheep's wool on head and legs is not
  drawn; variants (cat, rabbit, villager profession) use the default skin.
- An armor stand's own pose (`Pose.PoseIndex` in its saved data) is not
  read. Animations are found by name, so an entity animated under another
  name (donkey: horse) stays unposed.
- Light-blue faces flicker more in Simple graphics (lightning material).
- Many mobs (water, flying, projectiles) are unchecked in game. Checked:
  armor stand, chicken, cow, creeper, witch, wolf, pig, polar bear, turtle,
  camel, frog, wandering trader, stray, zombie, drowned, horse family.

## Tried
- Borrowing a whole animation family by bone fit: gave traders, strays and
  polar bears the sheep's head offset; removed.
- Animations whose bones are not all in the model: a horse's legacy setup
  moved the new head.
