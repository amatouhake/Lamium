# L-367 Game-like controls on the recipe windows; wanted items down to 0 by the wheel

Kind: Design
Status: The wheel to 0 seen in game and settled; the controls' look is open again (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-20)

With the recipe view and the plan window drawn as the game's window
(L-366), Lamium's dark tabs, buttons and switches on them stood out: an
off button or switch was a dark block on the light window (maintainer
2026-11-19, from screenshots). The wanted stepper's minus took an item out
at one, but the wheel and the wanted key's wheel stopped at one.

## Decided
- On the game's window the shared controls take the game's look (DESIGN
  "Controls"), measured from the game's inventory recipe book: raised
  buttons with dark text, an on button keeping its green face (not the
  game's pressed look, which tells on from off less clearly); tabs as
  blocks inside the window's heading, the chosen one joined to it (the
  game's tabs stand outside the window, where there is no room here);
  the switch keeps its shape with the game's lighter track.
- The wheel takes a wanted item to 0, shown faint; it goes out once the
  pointer leaves its row or the wanted key is up (RECIPES.md).
- Tried without a mockup first, in game (maintainer 2026-11-19).

## Open
- The controls' look (seen 2026-11-20): icon tabs too small on the band
  and in the recipe view; the band's half-unit rule too thin; the
  favourite and wanted buttons too low, silent and green for a game
  button; tabs joined to one white face read as joined to everything,
  make the title line redundant, and the unchosen ones are too dark.
  Lamium plays no UI sound anywhere yet.
