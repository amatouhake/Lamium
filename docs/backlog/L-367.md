# L-367 Game-like controls on the recipe windows; wanted items down to 0 by the wheel

Kind: Design
Status: Tabs on the top edge and the on/off buttons seen and kept; the windows' density and lists wait for a direction (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-20)

With the recipe view and the plan window drawn as the game's window
(L-366), Lamium's dark tabs, buttons and switches on them stood out: an
off button or switch was a dark block on the light window (maintainer
2026-11-19, from screenshots). The wanted stepper's minus took an item out
at one, but the wheel and the wanted key's wheel stopped at one.

## Decided
- Second look (maintainer 2026-11-20, after seeing the first): the
  windows' tabs are the game's, outside the windows on their top edge
  (the window starts 19 units down, its heading one line: name, pager,
  the two buttons; the plan window has no title); unchosen tabs in the
  slots' grey; pictures 16 units in the windows, 14 on the band, whose
  tabs are 14 high (one row of the band's list fewer at 360 high) over a
  one-unit rule. Favourite and wanted stay Lamium's joined buttons, green
  when on, with the switch's lighter off face (to see whether that
  settles them before trying the game's button). No sound for now. Row
  buttons stay raised.
- The wheel takes a wanted item to 0, shown faint; it goes out once the
  pointer leaves its row or the wanted key is up (RECIPES.md); seen and
  settled 2026-11-20.

## Open (seen 2026-11-20)
- The band's chosen picture tab: its green line overlaps the picture.
- The windows are crowded: no margins, the heading's name cut short, the
  pager beside the buttons; fewer recipes a page may be right.
- Raised buttons in rows (Gather, Make) without room, silent; the
  switch's black edge too dark on the light window; tab pictures not the
  game's shape; the "in use" mark of a chosen recipe.
- Gather and make lists: no overview of what is needed and gathered in
  all, names cut, an old Lamium tip; the make list does not show what
  turns into what. Pages turn only by the wheel.
- Whether to keep refining or first compare the player-visible features
  of Java recipe viewers (PROVENANCE: JEI and EMI, reference only).
