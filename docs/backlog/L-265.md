# L-265 Schematic placement assist: place the ghost's block in its state

Kind: Ready (strong model)
Status: Paused until L-421's aiming by the drawn boxes is settled (2026-11-28); off by default

A mode in which using a block on a ghost places the schematic's block there
in the schematic's state. The contract (behavior, code, what the traces
found) is [PLACEMENT-ASSIST.md](../PLACEMENT-ASSIST.md); this file keeps the
build order.

## Decided
- Everything in PLACEMENT-ASSIST.md "Behavior" (maintainer 2026-11-28):
  faked look for one placement, first ghost on the ray clicked from a real
  neighbor at a chosen click, auto pick, toggle + HUD + settings row,
  holding use keeps placing, a toast says why nothing was placed.
- From the fifth trace: faked placements need no rate limit when the move
  input is turned with the look; the trigger for a ghost with air behind is
  `GameMode::useItem`; `getPlacementBlock` with the relative click chooses.
- The click of a held press: the build context is cleared of its build
  direction for the assist's call, and a differing sent click is corrected
  and logged (to confirm in game, step 2).

- Target (maintainer 2026-11-28, after the second run): the ghost under
  the crosshair, the first on the ray, never one behind it; placing the
  vanilla way is no option, as ghosts hide the faces it would aim at. The
  code still walks past unplaceable ghosts until this is rebuilt.
- Rebuild (maintainer 2026-11-28, one of the four schematic problems in
  L-421): aim by the drawn boxes (L-421) so inner ghosts are reached;
  never fail silently, always say why; before the press, show the aimed
  ghost and whether it can be placed. To find out in game: whether the
  server accepts a click on a face turned away from the player (decides
  which ghosts can be placed), and why placing sometimes failed after a
  wrong block was broken.
