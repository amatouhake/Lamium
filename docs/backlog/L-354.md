# L-354 Drags that outlive a quick click or a focus loss in the settings

Kind: Ready (Bug, small)
Status: Open, not started

The 2026-11-16 pre-release review fixed held scroll bars in the settings
screen (`64114351`): the main branch and the calculator now handle a press
before the release that follows it within one frame, and a held scroll bar
is let go when the screen closes or the window loses focus. Two cases of
the same kind were left.

## Decided
- HUD layout editor: its branch of `SettingsScreen.cpp` `render` still
  processes the release before the press, on purpose according to its
  comment. A press and release within one frame (a quick click at a low
  frame rate) on the scroll bar of a contents popover leaves the bar held.
  Keep what the comment protects and let the popover's bar go.
- Settings table: a slider held while the window loses focus (Alt+Tab,
  button released in another window) stays held until the next click.
  `cancelInputCapture()` lets go of the scroll bar only; let go of the
  slider (`table_view::release()`) there too.

## Next
Read the HUD editor branch's comment and what depends on the order, then
fix both and add a pure test where the order lives in a header
(`ScrollDrag.h`, `PaneScroll.h`).
