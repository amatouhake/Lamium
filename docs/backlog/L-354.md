# L-354 Drags that outlive a quick click or a focus loss in the settings

Kind: Ready (Bug, small)
Status: Fixed in code; the DLL was not built where it was written and nothing was seen in game

The 2026-11-16 pre-release review fixed held scroll bars in the settings
screen (`64114351`): the main branch and the calculator handle a press
before the release that follows it within one frame, and a held scroll bar
is let go when the screen closes or the window loses focus. Two cases of
the same kind were left and are fixed here.

## Decided
- HUD layout editor: its branch of `SettingsScreen.cpp` `render` ends a
  drag before the press that follows it, so a click right after a drag
  starts fresh. The screen now remembers which came last between two frames
  (`scroll_drag::PressOrder`, `ScrollDrag.h`): a release queued after the
  press (a quick click at a low frame rate) runs after it and ends what
  the press began. That lets go of a contents popover's scroll bar, and
  also of an element or scale handle taken by such a click, which used to
  follow the pointer until the next click.
- Settings table: `cancelInputCapture()` lets go of a held slider
  (`table_view::release()`) with the scroll bar, so a slider held over
  Alt+Tab no longer follows the pointer afterwards.
