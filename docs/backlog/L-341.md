# L-341 Shared widgets for repeated controls

Kind: Refactor (strong model; no behavior change except where copies differ)
Status: Built, not checked in game: every control and the page shape are in

Screens carried their own copies of common controls (the 2026-11-14 review:
the press-twice button 8, the text field 8, the stepper 7 variants, the
scroll bar 5). Each now lives once in `src/ui/ListViewWidgets.h` on top of
`Widgets.h`; the rules are in DESIGN.md "Colors", "Controls" and "Screens",
not repeated here. `scripts/Check-UiTokens.ps1` (baseline
`scripts/UiTokenBaseline.txt`) only lets literal and hand-drawn button counts
shrink.

## Decided
- Palette: the tokens in DESIGN "Colors". `info` is `.62,.83,1` for every
  reference blue; the schematic "wrong block" reds are `mismatch`; the
  near-yellows (hunger trace, schematic wrong state) are `warning`; black is
  `shade` with the opacity at the call; schematic corners are
  `cornerOne`/`cornerTwo`; game-sampled colors and the preview's frame are in
  `palette::vanilla::`.
- Buttons: `drawButton`/`drawButtonBox` (plain, primary) and
  `drawDangerButton`. White text, a white wash on hover, on/selected draws as
  primary, disabled is plain with faint text. A destructive button looks
  plain until pointed at (an always visible red Reset drew the eye), then
  `dangerEdge` `.85,.33,.3`; armed it fills `danger`. The Open button keeps
  its key-cap look to match the key cell beside it.
- HUD layout: Done primary, Reset destructive; the game's own HUD parts
  (frame, legend) are `info`.
- Game language: the shared Open button with the current language faintly
  beside it; the row opens the list (click, Enter, Space).
- Pages that replace the rows (`RowPages.h`) share one heading
  (`drawPageHeading`), open one at a time, close on a click outside, and on
  focus loss only calm (end a drag, disarm). The tool views share their
  header (`drawToolHeader`) and docked frame (`drawDockedFrame`).
- Key capture cell: the row's selection frame is enough; the cell keeps its
  light accent fill without a frame of its own.
- Esc while typing drops the edit everywhere; Enter and a click outside keep
  it.
- Waypoints list "Add here" adds at once without the name prompt but takes
  the last "temporary" choice like the key and map prompts.
- Steppers are one box with end cells (enchantment counts, map height and
  coordinates, schematic save corners and HUD scale included); text fields
  and scroll bars have one look each.
- Schematic save: Save onto an existing file arms Overwrite (destructive,
  second press replaces); Stop while saving is a plain button.

## Open
- The schematic save corners and the enchantment levels on the settings
  page take typed values (digits; Enter/Tab keep, Esc drops). The panel
  beside the anvil only steps: typing there would take keys from the game.
- The world map's side list has no scroll bar (L-342).
- No token yet (not reference colors): the calculator's syntax colors, the
  world map's ground, the waypoint distance text (`.84,.86,.85`), the move
  target's gray, the switch and slider shades in Widgets.cpp.
