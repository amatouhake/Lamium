# L-360 Take items out of the item list in creative

Kind: Design, then Research
Status: Open, where and how agreed; key and count open

With the Recipes setting on, the item list beside the inventory only looks
items up. In creative, or where cheats are allowed, a player expects to take
an item out of such a list (maintainer, 2026-11-18, after the first check of
[L-359](L-359.md)).

## Decided
- Items no creative tab holds (water, lava) join the list after the
  creative ones by a setting: always, or only where items can be taken
  out (the default) (maintainer 2026-11-20, [L-371](done/L-371.md)).
- Taking is an action of its own in the key settings (rebindable), acting
  on the item under the pointer like R, U and A; the presses on the list
  stay as they are (maintainer 2026-11-24).
- Both one item and a stack can be taken (maintainer 2026-11-24).
- Allowed in creative, and in survival for a player with cheats on and
  operator rights (the permission check teleport uses, `map/Teleport.h`).
  In creative the item comes onto the pointer by the creative inventory's
  own action; in survival by `/give`, into the inventory (maintainer
  2026-11-24). Water and lava can only arrive by `/give`.
- Where it is not allowed a press does nothing and the key hints do not
  name it (maintainer 2026-11-24).

## Known
- Taking an item from the creative inventory's list is the screen's
  `_handleAutoPlace(amount, "recipe_nature", index)`, which the SDK
  provides; the server sees `CraftCreative` (craft_trace, local world,
  2026-11-18). The index is the item's place in the game's own tab.
- The map sends commands with `CommandRequestPacket` (`WorldMap.cpp`).
- The middle mouse button is free on the item screens; Shift and Ctrl with
  a left press already mean "as many as divide" and "craft onto the
  pointer".

## Open
- The default key (maintainer still choosing; the middle button is one
  candidate).
- How one and a stack are told apart: the maintainer reads Shift as "more",
  so "press: one, Shift: a stack" fits that reading; another is two actions
  (take one, take a stack), each with its own key.

## Research (after the choices)
- Whether the creative collections (`recipe_nature` and the other tabs)
  exist on screens other than the inventory (chest, crafting table,
  furnace); if not, how the item reaches the player there.
- Map a listed key to its tab and index; what an entry with data (potion,
  rocket, L-363) becomes through either path.
- A server: `CraftCreative` and `/give` with operator rights.
- A new capability, a GAME-UPDATES.md entry and `versionSensitiveAllowed()`.
