# L-360 Take items out of the item list in creative

Kind: Design, then Research
Status: Open, not started

With the Recipes setting on, the item list beside the inventory only looks
items up. In creative, or where cheats are allowed, a player expects to take
an item out of such a list (maintainer, 2026-11-18, after the first check of
[L-359](L-359.md)).

## Known
- Taking an item from the creative inventory's list is the screen's
  `_handleAutoPlace(amount, "recipe_nature", index)`, which the SDK
  provides; the server sees `CraftCreative` (craft_trace, local world,
  2026-11-18). The index is the item's place in the game's own tab.

## Open
- What takes an item: which press, how many (one, a stack), and how it
  lives beside a press that opens recipes.
- Where it is allowed: creative only, or also survival with cheats on, and
  what shows where it is not.
- How the item arrives: the creative inventory's own action (above; it
  needs the item's index in the game's tab, a capability of its own and a
  check on a server) or a command, which needs operator rights there.

## Next
Agree the presses and where it is allowed, then research how the creative
inventory hands an item to the player.
