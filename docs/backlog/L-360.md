# L-360 Take items out of the item list in creative

Kind: Design, then Research
Status: Open, "All" and "can be made" agreed; take one and the left lists open

With the Recipes setting on, the item list beside the inventory only looks
items up. In creative, or where cheats are allowed, a player expects to take
an item out of such a list (maintainer, 2026-11-18, after the first check of
[L-359](L-359.md)).

## Decided
- Items no creative tab holds (water, lava) join the list after the
  creative ones by a setting: always, or only where items can be taken
  out (the default) (maintainer 2026-11-20, [L-371](done/L-371.md)).
- Allowed in creative, and in survival for a player with cheats on and
  operator rights (the permission check teleport uses, `map/Teleport.h`).
  In creative the item comes by the creative inventory's own action; in
  survival by `/give`, always into the inventory. Water and lava can only
  arrive by `/give` (maintainer 2026-11-24).
- The presses (maintainer 2026-11-24, after trying the game's own):
  - "Can be made" keeps its presses: Shift + press places as many as
    divide, always. It is the list for crafting.
  - "All" is not for crafting: its Shift + press no longer places (the
    maintainer found "places where it can be made, else shows the
    recipes" hard to read). Where taking is allowed it puts a stack into
    the inventory, as Shift does in the game's creative list; elsewhere it
    shows the recipes, as a press does. RECIPES.md changes with the build.
  - Take one: an action of its own in the key settings, the middle button
    by default, acting on the item under the pointer. One item, as pick
    block gives one in the world; in creative onto the pointer, by `/give`
    into the inventory. Where taking is not allowed it does nothing and
    the key hints do not name it.
  - Left, right and Ctrl + press stay as they are.

## Known
- Taking an item from the creative inventory's list is the screen's
  `_handleAutoPlace(amount, "recipe_nature", index)`, which the SDK
  provides; the server sees `CraftCreative` (craft_trace, local world,
  2026-11-18). The index is the item's place in the game's own tab.
- Only the crafting screen (inventory, crafting table) and the furnaces
  override `_isInCreativeContainer` (SDK 26.60.4): the creative list's
  collections live there, not on a chest's screen.
- The map sends commands with `CommandRequestPacket` (`WorldMap.cpp`).
- Bedrock in creative (maintainer in game, 2026-11-24): its list gives a
  stack onto the pointer to a left press, one to a right press, a stack
  into the inventory to Shift with either, nothing to the middle button.
  In the world the middle button gives one into the main hand, with Shift
  too; with Ctrl one with its block data. `/give` gives 1 when no count
  is named, up to 32767, into the inventory; a full one drops the rest
  (minecraft.wiki Commands/give).

## Open
- Where take one works: under "All" only, or under "can be made" too (and
  the favorites, the recipe view, the game's slots).
- Whether Shift + middle button takes a stack onto the pointer (the game's
  creative list does that with a left press).
- What Shift + press does in the favorites and the plan's list, which
  have no filter.
- Take one while the pointer holds another item: into the inventory, the
  pointer left alone (Lamium's presses never move what it holds).

## Research
- Whether `recipe_nature` and the other tabs take an item at the crafting
  table and the furnaces as at the inventory, and how an item reaches the
  player at a chest, whose screen has no creative list.
- Map a listed key to its tab and index; what an entry with data (potion,
  rocket, L-363) becomes through either path.
- A server: `CraftCreative` and `/give` with operator rights.
- A new capability, a GAME-UPDATES.md entry and `versionSensitiveAllowed()`.
