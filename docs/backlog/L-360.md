# L-360 Take items out of the item list in creative

Kind: Ready
Status: Open, seen in game; two-line hints and list-only take keys not seen

With the Recipes setting on, the item list beside the inventory only looks
items up. In creative, or where cheats are allowed, a player expects to take
an item out of such a list (maintainer, 2026-11-18, after the first check of
[L-359](L-359.md)).

## Decided
- Creative only, by the creative inventory's own calls (Known). `/give`
  is set aside for now (maintainer 2026-11-24): no survival with cheats.
  Water and lava stay out of the list, and their setting
  ([L-371](done/L-371.md)) is dropped: as the maintainer recalls, not
  even `/give` hands them out as items.
- Where creative cannot take (the game's own search hides the item, the
  furnace, a chest) a press says why in the shared notice box
  (`drawNotice`, DESIGN "Passing notices"): the game's search, or "not on
  this screen" (maintainer 2026-11-24).
- The presses (maintainer 2026-11-24, after trying the game's own):
  - "Can be made" keeps its presses: Shift + press places as many as
    divide, always. It is the list for crafting.
  - "All" is not for crafting: its Shift + press no longer places (read
    as unclear). Where taking is allowed it puts a stack into the
    inventory, as the game's creative list does; elsewhere it shows the
    recipes. RECIPES.md changes with the build.
  - Take one: an action of its own in the key settings, the middle button
    by default. One item, as pick block gives one in the world.
  - Take a stack: another action, Shift + middle button by default; the
    only way to fill the pointer with a stack, also under "can be made".
  - Both act on the item under the pointer in Lamium's lists and the
    recipe view, not the game's slots (maintainer 2026-11-24, after it), onto
    the pointer. Outside creative they do nothing and the hints omit them.
  - The favorites and the plan's list have no filter: their Shift + press
    follows the game mode, cheats not counted. In creative as "All" (a
    stack into the inventory; favorites there may be building blocks, the
    plan a schematic's materials); in any other mode as "can be made"
    (as many as divide), showing the recipes where the item cannot be
    made now, their red slots saying why. Decided per press.
  - The key hints say what Shift + press does in the current mode, on a
    second line under the first where one does not hold them.
  - Left, right and Ctrl + press stay as they are.

## Known (creative_trace, VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-24)
- The game's list: a right press is `_handleTakeAmount(1, cell)` onto the
  pointer, Shift `_handleAutoPlace(INT_MAX, cell)` into the inventory; the
  server sees `CraftCreative`. Lamium's own calls on a cell did the same
  (1 or 64 onto the pointer, a stack into the inventory) at the inventory
  and the crafting table; at the furnace nothing, and a chest's screen
  has no creative collection.
- A cell exists only for what the game's pane shows on this visit: a tab's
  collection fills once that tab is opened, `recipe_search` follows the
  search text. Tabs show one cell for a group.
- `refreshContainer(true)` (virtual) on `recipe_search`'s model, from the
  crafting screen's tick, fills it with all 1980 entries at once, the
  pane unchanged, from any tab or with the pane hidden; the cells stay for
  the visit. Each keys (StackKey) to its own entry, books and rockets by
  their data. With text typed in the game's search the refresh keeps to
  it (0 cells for "sss"). Writing the screen's tab fields is not needed.
  So the creative route: the cell keyed as the item, after a refresh where
  it has none.
- `/give` (`CommandRequestPacket`, as `WorldMap.cpp`) works on every
  screen, counts items not stacks and carries no data (a blank enchanted
  book); a potion by aux is fine. The client read cheats and commands as
  off while LeviLamina enabled them: the rights must come from the
  server's command list, as teleport's (`teleportListed`).
