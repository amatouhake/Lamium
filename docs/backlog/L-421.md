# L-421 Schematic ghost look and aiming by what is drawn

Kind: Design (strong model)
Status: Look settings agreed (2026-11-28); Ready items for them and aiming by the drawn boxes are next

How ghosts look, which are drawn, and that what is aimed at is what is
drawn. The placement assist
([L-265 Schematic placement assist: place the ghost's block in its state](L-265.md))
is rebuilt on top of this.

## Decided
- Purpose (maintainer 2026-11-28): mainly building a schematic in
  survival while gathering materials, at every size, usually bottom-up a
  layer at a time. Copying in creative is a printer's job, not this
  feature's; checking a build made without the schematic is possible but
  not the aim. Lamium's schematic need not follow other schematic mods.
  Phases: planning (where it goes, what it needs), gathering (Recipes and
  the plan), placing (with checking). Placing hurts most.
- The problems split four ways (maintainer 2026-11-28), first mixed up
  in the placing pain: materials before placing
  ([L-422 Schematic materials before placing, with their raw materials](L-422.md)),
  the ghosts' look (this item), placing by the schematic (L-265), and how
  complex the schematic is to work with
  ([L-423 Schematic in-world 3D interaction: touch the placement to change it](L-423.md)).
  Layer switching belongs to L-423; which layers are drawn, here.
- No stages or modes (maintainer 2026-11-28, after first agreeing to
  Planning and Building stages): a stage would only name a default look,
  every action and the materials work the same in both, and it would add
  a HUD state, a key and an offer.
- This item's problems: ghosts hide the real blocks, overlapping ghosts
  leave unclear which one is aimed at, and the layer above sits at the
  player's body and head height while building bottom-up.
- Above the current layer: not drawn by default; a held key shows the
  whole placement.
- Looks are chosen in game from a trial build (`ghost_look_trial`, F8),
  kept for later comparisons. Results (VALIDATION-LOG "L-421 ghost look
  trial", "L-421 shrunk ghost trial"): outlines only read badly;
  translucency reads better than tinted faces (what lies behind shows;
  50% and 30% alike) but swaps drawing order with the viewing position,
  and at 80% size faces come and go with distance. Tinted at 60% size
  reads best, 80% fairly well; the outline alone tells a ghost from a
  block. Size alone did not make finding where to place easier.

- Look settings (maintainer 2026-11-28): a "look" group for one ghost's
  drawing. Faces: tint (as today), size as a slider 50-100% in 10% steps
  (100 default; block-entity blocks such as chests shrink too), opacity
  (experimental and not recommended: readable, but drawing order and
  faces at a distance are unstable; offered for trying and feedback).
  Lines: width and distance. Mistake marks stay with Check; which ghosts
  are drawn is no look setting. Defaults keep today's look.
- Aiming by the drawn boxes comes with the size setting, so a shrunk
  ghost is never aimed at by its full cell.
- Saved looks come after the look settings are built: a few good looks
  for given purposes are picked in game, then switched together (not a
  new settings format). Opacity stays out of them.

- Line width as a slider (maintainer 2026-11-28), replacing Thin /
  Normal / Thick / None: 0 none, 1 game lines (one screen pixel at any
  distance), then bars 1/64, 2/64, 3/64... block wide; the value reads
  "1 px", "1/64" and so on. Saved under a new key; the old choice maps
  thin 1, normal 2, thick 3, none 0, so nobody's look changes.

## Open
- The line slider's top end (3/64 or 4/64 block).
- Aiming by the drawn boxes: with shrunk ghosts the ray passes between
  them to an inner ghost (ghosts walled in by others) and to real blocks
  behind, so what is aimed at is what is seen. Pure ray-against-box code
  with tests, shared with L-423's handles.
- Which ghosts are drawn while placing (the lowest unfinished layer, what
  can be placed now), how the rest show, whether real blocks are never
  hidden.
