# L-421 Schematic ghost look and aiming by what is drawn

Kind: Design (strong model)
Status: Look settings built as L-424 (2026-11-28); saved looks and which ghosts are drawn are next

How ghosts look, which are drawn, and that what is aimed at is what is
drawn. The placement assist
([L-265 Schematic placement assist: place the ghost's block in its state](L-265.md))
is rebuilt on top of this.

## Decided
- Purpose (maintainer 2026-11-28): mainly building a schematic in
  survival while gathering materials, at every size, usually bottom-up a
  layer at a time. Copying in creative is a printer's job, not this
  feature's; checking a build made without the schematic is possible but
  not the aim. Lamium's schematic need not follow other schematic mods.
  Phases: planning (where it goes, what it needs), gathering (Recipes and
  the plan), placing (with checking). Placing hurts most.
- The problems split four ways (maintainer 2026-11-28), first mixed up
  in the placing pain: materials before placing
  ([L-422 Schematic materials before placing, with their raw materials](L-422.md)),
  the ghosts' look (this item), placing by the schematic (L-265), and how
  complex the schematic is to work with
  ([L-423 Schematic in-world 3D interaction: touch the placement to change it](L-423.md)).
  Layer switching belongs to L-423; which layers are drawn, here.
- No stages or modes (maintainer 2026-11-28, after first agreeing to
  Planning and Building stages): a stage would only name a default look,
  every action and the materials work the same in both, and it would add
  a HUD state, a key and an offer.
- This item's problems: ghosts hide the real blocks, overlapping ghosts
  leave unclear which one is aimed at, and the layer above sits at the
  player's body and head height while building bottom-up.
- Above the current layer: not drawn by default; a held key shows the
  whole placement.
- Looks were chosen in game from a trial build (F8, now removed).
  Results (VALIDATION-LOG "L-421 ghost look trial", "L-421 shrunk ghost
  trial"): outlines only read badly; translucency reads better than
  tinted faces but swaps drawing order with the view, and at 80% size
  faces come and go with distance. Tinted at 60% size reads best, 80%
  fairly well; the outline alone tells a ghost from a block. Size alone
  did not make finding where to place easier.
- Look settings and aiming by the drawn boxes (maintainer 2026-11-28):
  [L-424 Schematic ghost look settings: size, opacity, outline width](L-424.md)
  (size 50-100%, experimental opacity, outline width None / 1 px /
  1/64-4/64, a shrunk ghost aimed at only through its drawn box).
- Saved looks come after L-424 is checked in game: a few good looks for
  given purposes are picked in game, then switched together (not a new
  settings format). Opacity stays out of them.
