# L-421 Schematic building view: what is shown and aimed at while placing

Kind: Design (strong model)
Status: Purpose agreed, stages dropped, look trials seen (2026-11-28); look options and layer switching are next

The schematic is used through phases, and the placing phase hurts most.
This item designs how ghosts are shown and aimed at while placing, and the
placement assist ([L-265 Schematic placement assist: place the ghost's block in its state](L-265.md))
is rebuilt on top of it.

## Decided
- Purpose (maintainer 2026-11-28): mainly building a schematic in
  survival while gathering materials, at every size, usually bottom-up a
  layer at a time. Copying in creative is a printer's job, not this
  feature's; checking a build made without the schematic is possible but
  not the aim. Lamium's schematic need not follow other schematic mods.
- Phases: planning (where it goes, what it needs), gathering materials
  (Recipes and the plan cover it, maybe in parallel with placing), placing
  (with checking along the way and again at the end).
- The pain is placing: switching views by hand (layers) and finding where
  to place. Overlapping ghosts hide the real blocks, so players misclick
  and cannot predict what happens. Planning and placing likely want
  different views; today both use the same one, switched by hand.
- The placement assist aims at the ghost under the crosshair (L-265); it
  is not needed for the feature, but very handy.
- No stages or modes (maintainer 2026-11-28, after first agreeing to
  Planning and Building stages): a stage would only name a default look,
  every action and the materials work the same in both, and it would add
  a HUD state, a key and an offer. Planning and building may still want
  different looks; that is a question of look options (Open).

- Order (maintainer 2026-11-28): this item first, then the materials
  before placing ([L-422 Schematic materials before placing, with their raw materials](L-422.md)).
- Above the current layer: not drawn by default; the layer above sits at
  the player's body and head height while building bottom-up and would
  hide the real blocks again. A held key shows the whole placement.
- The current layer's look is chosen in game, not from a picture: a trial
  build switches between looks (faces tinted as today, outlines only, a
  fainter tint, translucent faces). Translucency, unpredictable over a
  whole dense build (SCHEMATIC.md "Tried"), is worth another look when
  only the cells near the player are drawn.
- Trial result (VALIDATION-LOG "L-421 ghost look trial"): outlines only
  read badly; translucency reads better than the tinted faces (what lies
  behind shows; 50% and 30% alike), but translucent ghosts swap drawing
  order with the viewing position (perhaps acceptable on one layer).
  Shrunk ghosts (VALIDATION-LOG "L-421 shrunk ghost trial"): tinted at
  60% reads best, 80% fairly well, translucent at 80% badly (faces come
  and go with distance); the outline alone tells a ghost from a block;
  finding where to place did not get easier from size alone.

## Open
- The placing view: which ghosts are drawn (the lowest unfinished layer,
  what can be placed now), how the rest show, whether real blocks are
  never hidden, how the aimed ghost and why it cannot be placed show.
- Look options: size and translucency read well in the trials, so more
  look settings are welcome; with many of them and different looks for
  planning and building, switching a set of settings together (settings
  profiles) is worth considering. Not decided; profiles change the
  settings format.
- Ghosts walled in by other ghosts: aiming by the shrunk boxes as drawn
  would let the ray pass between ghosts to an inner one (and to real
  blocks behind), so what is aimed at is what is seen.
- Layer switching: undecided whether the layer should follow the player
  (lowest unfinished layer nearby). What hurt may rather have been opening
  the menu and items whose effect is hard to imagine; an in-world control
  (switching the layer at the build itself) may feel closer. Unknown
  whether it is easier in use.
- Research: whether the server accepts a click on a face turned away from
  the player (decides which ghosts the assist can place).

## Next
The look trials are done (`ghost_look_trial`, kept for later
comparisons). Next: talk through look options and layer switching (menu,
