# L-422 Schematic materials before placing, with their raw materials

Kind: Design
Status: Open; after L-421 (maintainer 2026-11-28)

In the planning stage the player wants to know what a schematic needs
before placing it, and what those blocks are made from. Today the
materials appear only once a schematic is placed, and the raw materials
need a hand-over to the plan and another screen (the inventory's plan
window or the Notes).

## Decided
- Its own item, after [L-421 Schematic building view: what is shown and aimed at while placing](L-421.md)
  (maintainer 2026-11-28).

## Open
- Where the list shows: the Files tab next to the preview (the file is
  already read there), and how much of the Materials tab's view it reuses.
- The raw-material view without leaving the schematic screen, and handing
  a file's materials to the plan before it is placed.

## Next
A conversation with the maintainer once L-421 is settled, then a mockup.
