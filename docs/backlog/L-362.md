# L-362 What a recipe gives back

Kind: Design
Status: Open, not started

A cake takes three buckets of milk and gives the buckets back; the recipe
view shows only the cake, and a plan counts the buckets as used up
(maintainer, 2026-11-18, after the first check of [L-359](L-359.md)).

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20, [L-371](done/L-371.md))
- Built: what a recipe gives back, shown and credited, and how many to
  make so that little is left over.

## Known
- The index keeps what comes with a product (`Recipe::extra`, a cake's
  three buckets); nothing shows or credits it.

## Open
- How the amount that leaves least over is offered (a choice per wanted
  item, or the plan rounding up by itself), and how spare output shows.
- How the view shows it: beside the product, or as a mark on the
  ingredient that comes back.
- Whether a plan credits it: one bucket then serves many cakes, which
  changes what is left to gather.
