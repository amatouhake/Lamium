# L-168 Trade finder: reroll a villager until a wanted trade appears

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model).
Status: Waiting for the maintainer's choices below; not chosen for building, no release.

Find a wanted trade (for example a librarian's enchanted book at a given
level and price) by resetting a villager's profession again and again, for
every profession, inside Lamium's UI. Separate from L-166 (trading once a
good villager is found). A never-traded villager loses its profession when
its workstation is broken and rolls new trades when it takes one again. On
Bedrock the client receives every trade of every level when the trade screen
opens (L-129), so level-5 offers can be checked at level 1.

## Decided
- The loop is wanted (maintainer 2026-10-12): with the player looking at a
  villager and its workstation, Lamium repeats break, place, open the trade
  screen, read (L-129's parsing), close, until a match or a stop. Every step
  goes through vanilla's own actions and waits for authoritative results.
  Stops on any player input, a missing workstation item, a different
  villager, a traded (locked) villager, low durability (L-62), world or
  dimension change. Default off; help text warns that servers may treat it
  as automation. (The assist stage, announcing a match with toast and sound
  while the player rerolls by hand, is the same matcher without the loop.)
- The wanted-offer list is per world or server, keyed like the map's
  per-world data, not shared.

## Open
- How wanted offers are entered (agent: a picker from the game's item and
  enchantment lists); whether matches are kept as a short log.
- The offer matcher is not written until these are decided (recommendations
  are the agent's, 2026-11-12):
  - Level: exact, or that level and above. Recommended: and above.
  - Price cap: the first input's count as shown (after discounts and demand)
    or the base price. Recommended: as shown, said in the help text.
  - A book with two enchantments: matches if any wanted enchantment is on it,
    or only if all are. Recommended: any.
- Villager identity for L-166 favourites: `Actor::getOrCreateUniqueID()`
  links in SDK 26.60.4 (header only). Check whether the client's value equals
  the server's persistent id and survives a rejoin before choosing
  per-villager favourites.

## Next
Research before a spec: when trades are rolled on the current game (on taking the
profession or on first opening), how long a villager takes to claim a placed
workstation, and whether a server behaves the same.
