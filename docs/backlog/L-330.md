# L-330 Radar: mob icons that draw wrongly, then icons by default

Kind: Design
Status: Faces rebuilt from the turned model and seen in game (2026-11-26, `1caa29ca`); horse colors, large mobs and the default wait for the maintainer

Some mobs' icons on the radar did not draw correctly (maintainer
2026-11-13), so the radar keeps its Experimental badge.

## Decided
- If the icons can be made right, the radar's default changes from dots to
  icons (`map.radarFaces`, off today). The default changes only once the
  icons are confirmed in game (maintainer 2026-11-13).
- How a face is seen (maintainer 2026-11-26, `faceRecipe` in
  `features/map/MapFaces.h`): the front of the "head" with the parts
  hanging from it, turned as the model is; tack and parts the game hides
  left out; a kind without a head shows the part where its face is
  (squid's body, the slime's cube, wither's middle head); long necks
  (llamas, camels) cut two rows below the face's width; fish and parrots
  from the side; every mob at the same texel size, so outlines match and
  sizes follow the models.

## Known
- The `radar_icon_probe` sweep writes every renderer's face once per world
  to logs/radar-sweep.* (111 faces with `1caa29ca`). The game's model files
  (`data/resource_packs/vanilla*/__brarchive`, a flat archive of JSON and
  PNG) match what the renderers hold: a part's pivot is its offset from its
  parent's with y the other way, a root's from y 24; rotations keep the
  file's signs in radians.
- A face is per renderer, not per mob: every horse gets the white horse's
  face, every cat, llama or parrot one color.
- Large models give large icons at one texel size: squid and glow squid
  12x16, ghast and happy ghast 16x16, strider 16x14 (maintainer: the squid
  looks big beside the others).

## Open
- Horses (and other kinds with colors): one face per color, read from the
  mob, or one common color.
- Large mobs: halve faces wider or taller than 12 texels (squid 6x8, ghast
  8x8), or keep them at the model's size.
- Whether the faces are now right enough to make them the default.
