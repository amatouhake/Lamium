# L-413 The plan's held column as what is really held

Kind: Design
Status: Open, not started

The plan's held column shows each row's share of what is held, so it stops
at the need: holding 80 of 64 reads "64". The tips' "余り" line says what
the whole plan leaves unused ([L-387](done/L-387.md)), but the maintainer
would rather the column could show the real count later (2026-11-27).

## Decided
- For now the column keeps the share; the spare is in the tip only.

## Known
- Stock goes to the wanted entries from the top down
  (`TargetPlanning`, `PlanTargets.h`): what one row uses is gone for the
  rows below it, and a row is done by its share.
- "Wanted": one item can be on two rows, a feature's (the schematic's)
  and the player's apart. A real total on both rows reads as if twice
  that were held, and the lower row can read "80 / 64" while not done.
- "To gather": one row per item, but a group and its member can both be
  rows (oak logs wanted, chests needing logs of any wood); the group's
  held is its members' together, so the oak logs count on both.
- `unusedStock` (`RawView.h`) gives what the plan leaves unused, by item.

## Open
- How the column shows a real count where an item has several rows: on
  its first row only, a mark on rows that share it, or the rows of one
  item joined into one (the schematic's and the player's).
- Whether "to gather" follows the same rule for a group and its members.
- Whether done still follows the share while the column shows the total.
