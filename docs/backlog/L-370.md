# L-370 What the plan is for and how it shows

Kind: Design
Status: Rebuilt as decided and seen in game (`14608c13`, VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-20); proposals open

The plan window was built in steps (L-359, L-367). Seen as a whole on
2026-11-20 it was tidy but unclear about what it is for. Rebuilt around
the wanted items it worked, yet read badly: rows big for what they said,
the same thing said three ways, numbers out of column, a breakdown with
no style of its own, recipe stepping that hid the next one, an order
whose meaning was unclear (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-20). Each question is
now settled on its own with mockups (`docs/demos/plan-*.html`).

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20)
- Around the wanted items, as PLAN.md lays out: their tab with a press
  opening the breakdown, "to gather" the shopping list for all, "to make"
  the work. Held stock goes to them in the order added; no reordering.
- A breakdown: indented on a sunk face, read only, steps as slots with
  the station and the choice's state (a press goes to "How to make").
- Choices are the player's for all plans and worlds, not per wanted item:
  a group's member and an item's recipe, set in one tab, "作り方" ("How to
  make"); "お気に入り" keeps its name. A group not chosen stays "any"
  (held members together, nothing traced past it). Gather or make stays
  item by item.
- In "How to make": "any" and "automatic" look like the other options; a
  line's words stand by its item, not right-aligned; which lines show
  (in the current plan only, or all chosen before) is a switch, not tabs.
- In "How to make" a group's members are a grid of slots, named by the
  game's tip (no name line). A slot that takes any of several items, in
  the recipe view and a breakdown, is one named item ("planks (any)"):
  its picture the chosen member, or turning through them; its tip the
  name and the members as slots (fixes the L-369 tip).
- How far choices reach (maintainer 2026-11-20): the Recipes feature as a
  whole. Placing ("can be made", the place button, Mass Craft) follows
  "How to make" and falls back to what is held when it cannot. The list
  stays a tab of the Recipes feature's window (no title). In the
  recipe view an item with several recipes is chosen by its check box
  ([L-379](done/L-379.md)), also outside the plan; with the plan off the
  window holds only "How to make".
- A group: a "*" inside its icon (a wildcard; the icon turning through the
  members says the rest; [demos/any-mark.html](../demos/any-mark.html),
  2026-11-21); its name the tag's or "<lead> etc.", the lead its plainest
  member (sand, oak, cobblestone); in "How to make" its picture the member most held,
  else the lead (maintainer 2026-11-21; not member icons side by side).
- The window takes the book-open width; with the book closed the bands
  narrow while it is open.
- A one-member recipe is left out beside the any-member one covering it.
- "Automatic" (maintainer 2026-11-21): the station's order stays (crafting
  first: Mass Craft makes many there, a stonecutter 64 at most); within a
  station and priority the recipe with fewer steps from what is gathered
  wins, then the name. Built, not checked in game.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21, [demos/plan-tabs.html](../demos/plan-tabs.html))
- The window's tabs as one: "to gather" under "still to gather" and
  "gathered", the band "held/needed" alike; "to make" with "can make now"
  and "needed" columns and a bar; "How to make" states on one line, one
  arrow, aligned columns, "automatic" as its recipe's slots; on the game's
  grey a scrollbar of a sunk track and a raised thumb. Built, not checked.

## Proposed (maintainer 2026-11-20, not decided)
- Highlight the items the next step uses, as Enchantment Order does.
- Lay out, or lay and craft, a step to make from the plan (a button on
  the band's "to make", or a key).
- Step states (agreed in [L-371](L-371.md); decided 2026-11-21 from
  [demos/plan-step-states.html](../demos/plan-step-states.html)): can
  make, partly, missing, held, in that order under headings, a partly
  row's "now/needed" in items at the right, short inputs in the game's
  red, held steps faint at the end. The band's "to make" alike, in its
  short form. Built, not checked in game.
