# L-370 What the plan is for and how it shows

Kind: Design
Status: Decided (2026-11-20), not built

The plan window was built in steps (L-359, L-367): wanted items in, "to
gather" and "to make" out, stores counted. Seen as a whole on 2026-11-20
it looks tidy but unclear about what it is for and why its parts are
split as they are (maintainer).

## Known (seen 2026-11-20)
- How much of each wanted item is still needed and how much is gathered
  is hard to see: the lists are by material and by step, not by target.
- Gather or make is switched item by item ("gather" / "make" on a row).
- The whole's line exists only on "to gather", in the room under the
  tabs.
- A make row does not show at a glance what turns into what.
- A group member picked on "to gather" cannot be changed or put back
  there; on "to make" it can.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20)
- The plan is built around the wanted items (way A): the wanted tab
  shows how far each is; opening a row shows its own breakdown, where
  its choices are made. "To gather" stays the shopping list for all,
  "to make" the work to do.
- A wanted row: the finished items held against those wanted ("0 / 1")
  and a bar of how much of what it takes is gathered, a held part made
  on the way counting.
- Held stock shared by several wanted items goes to them from the top of
  the list down: the list's order is their priority. Totals are as now.
- Items a feature hands over (a schematic's) stand one by one among the
  player's own, as now.
- "To make": the steps that can be made now first, the others under them
  faint.
- A wanted row's breakdown: its own steps in the order to make them,
  each "inputs -> product", then what it needs gathered, have / need. A
  step's row chooses its recipe and members; a material's or a part's row
  switches gather or make, and an item set to "gather" says so there.
- The order (priority) changes with up and down buttons on the row under
  the pointer, handed items too. Gather or make stays item by item.
- Only the breakdown chooses. "To make" is the list of work: no choosing
  there; a press on a step opens the breakdown of the wanted item that
  uses it.
- A step's row, in the breakdown and in "to make", shows its inputs and
  its product in the game's slots (16-unit items, the count at the lower
  right) as "slot slot -> slot", with the station; rows about 20 high.
