# L-370 What the plan is for and how it shows

Kind: Design
Status: Rebuilt as decided (2026-11-20), not checked in game

The plan window was built in steps (L-359, L-367). Seen as a whole on
2026-11-20 it was tidy but unclear about what it is for. Rebuilt around
the wanted items it worked, yet read badly: rows big for what they said,
the same thing said three ways, numbers out of column, a breakdown with
no style of its own, recipe stepping that hid the next one, an order
whose meaning was unclear (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-20). Each question is
now settled on its own with mockups (`docs/demos/plan-*.html`).

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20)
- Around the wanted items: the wanted tab shows how far each is; a press
  opens its breakdown (its steps in the order to make them, then what it
  needs gathered). "To gather" is the shopping list for all, on the same
  columns, the whole as its first row. "To make" is the work: steps that
  can be made now first, the others faint; a press opens the breakdown
  that needs it.
- A wanted row is a table row: fold, icon, name, held, wanted, a bar of
  held against wanted (what is gathered is "to gather"'s); no percent. The player's own rows turn "wanted" into "- N +" and show
  remove only under the pointer; nothing moves. Handed items show their
  count and a small source mark.
- Held stock goes to wanted items in the order added (the player's own,
  then handed); no reordering (it changed little and took long). A row
  whose stock went to one above says so under the pointer.
- A breakdown is indented on a sunk face, steps and materials on one icon
  column, read only. A step shows as slots, inputs right-aligned to the
  arrow, product in a fixed column, with the station and its choice's
  state; a press on that state goes to the line in "How to make".
- Choices are the player's for all plans and worlds, not per wanted item:
  a group's member and an item's recipe, set in one tab, "作り方" ("How to
  make"); "お気に入り" keeps its name. A group not chosen stays "any"
  (held members together, nothing traced past it). Gather or make stays
  item by item.
- In "How to make": "any" and "automatic" look like the other options; a
  line's words stand by its item, not right-aligned; which lines show
  (in the current plan only, or all chosen before) is a switch, not tabs.
- In the recipe view each recipe stands on a raised face (L-369 look).

- In "How to make" a group's members are a grid of slots, named by the
  game's tip (no name line). A slot that takes any of several items, in
  the recipe view and a breakdown, is one named item ("planks (any)"):
  its picture the chosen member, or turning through them; its tip the
  name and the members as slots (fixes the L-369 tip). Looked at first
  (maintainer 2026-11-20): the
  game's own recipe book (Bedrock, seen 2026-11-20) never asks: it shows
  one member that suits what is held (a chest's planks from the logs
  carried; a furnace from cobblestone) and lays mixed members in; tidy,
  but it plans nothing ahead, so little to borrow. Other viewers, from
  public pages (2026-11-20, kept outside the repository): a slot that
  turns through members (JEI, REI); one named group with its members in
  the tip (EMI, newer JEI); a member chosen once for all and kept, with no
  list to review it (EMI only).

- How far choices reach (maintainer 2026-11-20): the Recipes feature as a
  whole. Placing ("can be made", the place button, Mass Craft) follows
  "How to make" and falls back to what is held when it cannot. The list
  stays a tab of the window, which is the Recipes feature's window, not
  the plan's (it has no title; "Stores" is not the plan's either). In the
  recipe view an item with several recipes shows its in-use mark (or
  "automatic") also outside the plan, and a press goes to its line; with
  the plan off the window holds only "How to make".
- A recipe of one member (a chest of crimson planks) is left out beside
  the recipe of any member that covers it.

## Proposed (maintainer 2026-11-20, not decided)
- "Automatic" takes the fewest steps in general, not only past a made
  input (`skipsStep`).
- "How to make" shows a group as several member icons, not one turning.
- Highlight the items the next step uses, as Enchantment Order does.
- Lay out, or lay and craft, a step to make from the plan (a button on
  the band's "to make", or a key).
