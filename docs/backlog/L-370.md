# L-370 What the plan is for and how it shows

Kind: Design
Status: The Notes tables and the panel's columns seen in game 2026-11-22

How the plan's window reads; each question settled with a mockup
(`docs/demos/plan-*.html`).

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20)
- Around the wanted items, as PLAN.md lays out: their tab with a press
  opening the breakdown, "to gather" the shopping list for all, "to make"
  the work. Held stock goes to them in the order added; no reordering.
- A breakdown: indented on a sunk face, read only, steps as slots with
  the station and the choice's state (a press goes to "How to make").
- Choices are the player's for all plans and worlds, not per wanted item:
  a group's member and an item's recipe, set in one tab, "作り方" ("How to
  make"); "お気に入り" keeps its name. A group not chosen stays "any"
  (held members together; tracing past it: L-386).
- In "How to make": "any" and "automatic" look like the other options; a
  line's words stand by its item, not right-aligned; which lines show
  (in the current plan only, or all chosen before) is a switch, not tabs.
- In "How to make" a group's members are a grid of slots, named by the
  game's tip (no name line). A slot that takes any of several items, in
  the recipe view and a breakdown, is one named item ("planks (any)"):
  its picture the chosen member, or turning through them; its tip the
  name and the members as slots (fixes the L-369 tip).
- Choices reach the whole Recipes feature: placing follows "How to make",
  else what is held; the recipe view's check box chooses too
  ([L-379](done/L-379.md)); with the plan off the window holds only it.
- A group: a "*" inside its icon (a wildcard; the icon turning through the
  members says the rest; [demos/any-mark.html](../demos/any-mark.html),
  2026-11-21); its name the tag's or "<lead> etc.", the lead its plainest
  member (sand, oak, cobblestone); in "How to make" its picture the member most held,
  else the lead (maintainer 2026-11-21; not member icons side by side).
- The window takes the book-open width (the bands narrow for it); a
  one-member recipe is left out beside the any-member one covering it.
- "Automatic" (maintainer 2026-11-21): the station's order stays (crafting
  first: Mass Craft makes many there, a stonecutter 64 at most); within a
  station and priority the recipe with fewer steps from what is gathered
  wins, then the name.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- From [demos/plan-tabs.html](../demos/plan-tabs.html): the tabs as one
  (gather headings, make columns and bar, How to make on one line, a sunk
  scrollbar) and the step states (can make, partly, missing, held, from
  [demos/plan-step-states.html](../demos/plan-step-states.html)). Seen in game.
- Done wanted items under "done", faint, window and band. The target column reads "needed" on every tab; "can make now" stays
  (cut to "今作…" in Japanese, accepted). Only column headings stay put;
  the switch rows and "All" scroll with the list.
- Counts ([demos/plan-numbers.html](../demos/plan-numbers.html)): the
  band shows what is left, done rows checked, the rest in the tip.
- The band's wanted rows: the count, the stepper under the pointer (five
  digits); a hand-added and a handed row of one item stay two rows.
- "How to make" offers "gather" beside "automatic" and the recipes (the
  breakdown's gather, same notes). Items usually gathered start as
  chosen "gather": leather, wool, ore-smelted items (one rule, 2026-11-22);
  more as found. Only the player's change is saved; presets
  show for the current plan, not under "all chosen before".

## Decided: the Notes tables (maintainer 2026-11-22)
- Today's order stays. What comes before the table (icon, name, a
  step's slots) takes the left 45% at most; the table the rest, short of
  the scrollbar by a margin, in columns that line up without the
  pointer ([demos/notes-table.html](../demos/notes-table.html) A: held,
  needed close, bar the rest, controls as wide as the tab's widest).
  A hand-added count: a faint sunk box typed with the search field's
  keys, the wheel steps it; "x" in its column. "To make": no names, no
  store mark, the station full size apart from the step. "All" a plain
  row; breakdown steps one indent in; "How to make" on the same halves;
  six-digit columns, a cut count whole in its tip; switches after words.
  The panel follows afterwards, keeping what it can.
