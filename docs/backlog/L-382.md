# L-382 One way into the Recipes window, and the panels around the game's window

Kind: Ready
Status: Built, not checked in game

The Recipes window began as the plan's and now holds the plan, "How to
make" and the stores. Its ways in grew one by one: "Open" on the plan's
panel, and with the plan off a "How to make" button by the search field
that ran into the item list. The stores' line sits under the left panel
though the layout cannot move it, and the layout is reached only from
the settings.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- One icon button beside the search field opens the window on its last
  tab and closes it again (green while open). "Open" on the plan's panel
  and the plan-off "How to make" button go; a press on a panel's row
  still opens the window on that row's tab.
- A second icon button beside it starts arranging the panels on the
  screen in use; the settings keep their way in too.
- The open container's "Counted as a store" switch moves onto the game
  window's title line, right of the title and left of the close button;
  the line under the left panel goes.
- "Bands" are called panels in every language ("パネル"), settings,
  help and documents alike.
- The search field, the two buttons and the highlight switch stay
  between the left and right panels: never over them. The row reads
  "open the window, arrange the panels | search field, highlight": the
  screen's controls left, the search's right, whatever the panels.
- Every button's picture is white and greys, green only when on; the
  layout button's is a divided square.
- The store switch on the title line is a button of the close button's
  size with a picture, green when counted, named in its tip.

## Decided: pictures and name (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- The window's button: a tabbed window; the window is "Notes" ("ノート"),
  its tip listing the tabs shown. The store button: a box with a check.

## Next

chest; the store button's place beside the close button.
