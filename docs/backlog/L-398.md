# L-398 The recipe key hints as a tooltip of their own

Kind: Ready
Status: Built, not checked in game

The key hints over the search row list every key of the Recipes screens
and take three lines in Japanese and English alike (VALIDATION-LOG
2026-11-24, after [L-360](done/L-360.md) added Shift and the take keys).
The maintainer finds them too long to stand there all the time.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-24)
- The bottom row gets a picture button with a question mark, right of the
  search highlight switch, drawn as the row's other picture buttons.
- Under the pointer it shows the keys as a two-column table in the game's
  tooltip box, as the search field shows its syntax: a key, what it does.
  The rows are today's hints: click (recipes), right click (uses), the
  favorite and wanted keys, Backspace (back, while a recipe is open),
  Shift + click (what it does in this game mode), the take keys in
  creative; keys by their current bindings, unbound ones left out.
- No standing hints line any more. What the thing under the pointer does
  (the plan window's "Wheel: look through the kinds" and the like, L-369)
  still shows over the search row as one small line, and so do the
  notices.

## Built
- `Picture::Keys` (a question mark) in the shared widgets; `keysButtonAt`
  at the row's end, the field 18 narrower for it. The tip's heading reads
  "Keys"; the table as decided.
