# L-123 FreeCamera stretches the worn elytra on the inventory player model

Kind: Bug, low priority
Status: Parked 2026-10-10 (maintainer) after two culling rounds; resume from the ideas in Next

With an elytra worn and FreeCamera on, the elytra on the player's 3D model
in the inventory screen draws simplified and stretched downward. The elytra
slot icon is not affected. Reproduce: wear an elytra, turn FreeCamera on,
move the camera away from the body, open the inventory.

## Decided
- Checked 2026-10-10: wrong when the body is out of view (camera near),
  right when the body is in view (camera far). So the inventory model
  reuses pose state that only the world render of the body updates.

## Next
Research which state (elytra wing animation) is left stale and whether it
can be refreshed for the inventory model alone. Ideas, not started:
- Find the entity render queue's own visibility step
  (`LevelRendererCamera::queueRenderEntities` and what it calls).
- Refresh the pose on the inventory model's side (the paper doll render
  path, `GeometryAtlas::DollRenderContextImpl::update`, `PaperDollData`).

## Tried
Both reverted; the elytra stayed stretched, so these camera tests are not
what skips the body's pose update:
- Keeping the body "visible" to `LevelRendererCamera::isAABBVisible`: never
  matched on the render thread.
- Keeping it visible to both `isAABBVisible` and `cullerIsVisible` on any
  thread: the point test was overridden, no change.
