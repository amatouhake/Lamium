# L-240 Death layout restore sometimes restores unexpectedly or wrongly

Kind: Bug / Research
Status: Open; waiting to see whether L-338's time limit stops the unexpected restores

Death layout restore sometimes acts after a death the player does not
recall ("did I die there?"), and when it restores, the result sometimes
looks wrong (maintainer, everyday use, 2026-10-22, builds not recorded).
Hard to reproduce: deaths and pickups are hard to observe. The feature
keeps its Experimental badge.

## Decided
- No fix before a concrete case.

## Next
Decide what to observe (which death a restore belongs to, the recorded
layout versus the pickups applied) and whether a bounded trace build or an
in-game log line per restore is the cheapest way to catch it.
