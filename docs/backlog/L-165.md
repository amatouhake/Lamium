# L-165 Mass Craft: craft a recipe many times at once

Kind: Design, then Research (strong model).
Status: Open; the maintainer's picture of it is written down, its controls and the dropping are not agreed or researched. No release

Turn what keeps arriving in the inventory into products without a press
per craft: while a key is held, lay in as many as divide evenly, craft,
throw the products out, and again (the maintainer's picture, 2026-11-18:
standing where items collect, logs into planks, iron into blocks).

## Decided
- Not the recipe view's Shift + "place": that lays in and leaves crafting
  to the player (maintainer 2026-11-18, after using a build where it also
  crafted). Nor a button in the recipe view at all: the view hides the
  inventory, which is what a mass craft is watched on.
- It lives on the crafting screen: a button there to hold, or a key of its
  own that acts while the pointer is over the output slot. It runs while
  held.
- A round lays in as many as divide evenly, crafts all the grid makes and
  throws the products out of the inventory.
- Wanted if it can be done: while it runs, some of an ingredient the
  inventory has too much of is dropped, so the other ingredients of the
  recipe can be picked up. Seen elsewhere without it: the inventory full
  of one ingredient, the others lying around as items, nothing crafted.
- A missing ingredient stops a round. Any of several items: the member
  picked in the wanted window, else the one most carried.
- Crafting from nearby chests is out.

## Known
Read with the `craft_trace` build in a local world (VALIDATION-LOG
2026-11-18):
- A click on a recipe in the game's book fills the grid with as much as
  divides evenly (64 logs into one cell; 60 of 64 ingots over six cells),
  one ordinary place request per cell, and sets the manager's selected
  recipe.
- Taking the output by a plain click is one craft request (`CraftRecipe`,
  one craft). One request can carry many: `CraftRecipeAuto` was seen with
  16 crafts (64 logs' planks: a stack of output) and with 7 (iron blocks
  from 64 ingots, straight from the inventory with an empty grid). Which
  press sends it is not settled: the screen's own calls for it are not
  among those Lamium can hook.
- Lamium can do both itself (trace builds, local world): a recipe laid in
  cell by cell with `_handleTakeAmount` and `_handlePlaceAll` was accepted
  every time, and one Shift take of the output (`_handleAutoPlace` on
  `crafting_output_items`) crafted all the grid held, as far as the
  inventory had room; a take that fits nothing asks the server for
  nothing. The output slot reads empty while a product shows: the
  manager's crafting recipe tells whether there is one.
- The SDK does not provide the screen's and the manager's "recipe
  selected" and "craft all" calls (`_handleRecipeSelect`,
  `handleRecipeSelect`, `autoCraftItem`) nor the client's request
  constructor. It does provide the slot calls Item transfer uses
  (`_handleAutoPlace`, `_handleTakeAmount`, `$_handleTakePlace`,
  `$_handlePlaceOne`) and `_evacuateCraftingGrid`.

## Open
- The button to hold or the key over the output slot, or both; the key's
  default; a setting of its own, off by default.
- Which recipe: the one lying in the grid when it starts (agent's reading
  of "over the output slot"), laid there by hand or with "place".
- Throwing out: always, or a choice to keep the products; where they go
  (the drop key's throw lands in front of the player, where they may be
  picked up again).
- Dropping a surplus: how much of which ingredient (agent: down to the
  recipe's own proportions, only when a round cannot be laid in and no
  slot is free), and how it is kept from being picked straight up again.
- Whether a "craft the last recipe again" key is wanted (agent: no).
- Stonecutter, loom, smithing and furnaces.
- Watch out for: leftovers (buckets, bottles, [L-362](L-362.md)) and
  servers limiting request rates.

## Next
Agree the open points above. Research then: whether the output can be
dropped straight from its slot (what the drop key does there) or has to
pass through the inventory, and how many rounds a second a server takes.
The crafting run without the throw is built (`RecipePlace.cpp`, `craft`)
and was seen in game (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-18); nothing starts it.
