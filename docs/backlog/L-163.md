# L-163 A Lamium-drawn inventory screen

Kind: Research, then Design (strong model).
Status: Open, not started; recorded for later.

Draw an inventory-like screen in Lamium and move items through the client
inventory path Lamium already uses without a screen (`inventory::game` moves,
as Auto Elytra and Hand Restock do). Such a screen could open in portals and
let the player move, with input routed differently from the vanilla screen.
Raised by the maintainer 2026-10-12 after L-149 (the server refuses opening
the vanilla inventory in a portal) and L-132 (moving with the vanilla
inventory open failed). Evidence: Auto Elytra swapped items while the player
stood in a portal (local world), and Hand Restock works on BDS.

## Open
- Costs and risks: slot rendering (Inventory HUD parts exist), click, right
  click split, shift click, drag spreading, number keys, armor and offhand
  slots, tooltips; 2x2 crafting is much harder and stays out at first; loss
  or duplication if a move is not confirmed from authoritative state;
  servers with their own anti-cheat may refuse; version sensitivity.

## Next
Suggested order: (1) a spike moving one item from a Lamium screen in a portal
and while walking, locally and on BDS; (2) if it holds, a minimal screen
(main inventory, hotbar, armor, offhand; click and shift click; no crafting);
(3) a mockup agreed before the real build.
