# L-183 Disregard selected schematic check mismatches

Kind: Design (strong model)
Status: Built on branch `l-190-waypoint-selection` (`41ace47e`), checked in game; design reopened 2026-11-15

Leave chosen expected-vs-actual differences out of the check results (a
circuit under construction, a deliberate change, an error in the file).
The schematic file and the placed blocks do not change; the strict
comparison stays available.

## Decided
- Stored per placement in its saved file, as an optional key in schematic
  coordinates, so moving or turning keeps it; kept across rejoining. Older
  builds that save the placement may drop it (2026-11-06).
- Undo one or all; stale entries are not applied; at most 64 per placement.
  No "temporarily" in the wording.
- Still standing from v2 (2026-11-15): a cell not counted is out of every
  count and the materials, like extras' "do not count", and keeps its
  ghost; an ignored difference counts as matching. Keys and the radial menu
  toggle the looked-at cell of the selected placement ("Show placement"
  moved to Placement); the target card says the place is ignored.

## Built
- `CheckIgnores.h` (kinds, list, saved form, staleness, `judgeIgnores`) and
  one counting rule in `Verify.h`/`Verification.h` that totals, progress,
  materials, HUD, the nearest-mistake key and marks all use.
- Check tab row and several-row actions, "Ignored: N" list with Undo, the
  action `ignorelookedblock` (appended, unbound) and its menu item.

## Chosen without asking (kept unless the new model changes them)
- A cell not counted leaves the materials whatever is there. Changed
  entries keep the rows; counts say "counting" until the next pass. English
  menu label "Ignore looked block" (fits the ring at a small GUI scale).
  Seen in game 2026-11-15 (`41ace47e`).
