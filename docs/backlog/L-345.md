# L-345 Shared tips and popup panels

Kind: Refactor (agreed 2026-11-15)
Status: Built, not checked in game

DESIGN "Controls" says tips look the same everywhere, but five functions draw
them, and floating panels use opacities from .8 to 1.0 (DESIGN gives .8 for
the screen). Follows [L-341](done/L-341.md): list how the copies differ,
agree, then one shared widget in `ListViewWidgets.h` and the DESIGN entry in
the same commit.

## Known
Tips (all `panel` .97 with an edge, padding 4):
- Settings: binding conflicts (`keyEdge`, `warning` when it warns), a cut
  stepper value, the version line (`SettingsTableView.cpp`).
- Enchantment steps (`EnchantSteps.cpp`), calculator key tips (one line,
  `dim` text) and the calculator's passing note (`accent` edge).
- Locked trades (`LockedTrades.cpp`) copy the game's own item tooltip (dark
  .92, purple edge, game font), like the container preview's frame.
Floating panels: settings prompts and the schematic radial .86, schematic
cards .9, calculator .88 (its copy menu and help .98), world map side panels
.92, its context menu .94, its menu .98, its top and bottom bars .85, HUD
editor toolbar and popovers 1.0, the screen and the anvil panels .8.

## Decided
- Two opacity tiers by what is under the panel (maintainer 2026-11-15, after
  seeing .97 on the radial and HUD editor): over the world `.8` like the
  settings screen (prompts, the schematic radial and its cards, the HUD
  editor's panels and popovers); over another Lamium panel `.97` (tips,
  menus, the world map's side panels, the calculator's copy menu and help).
  The world map's top and bottom bars stay `.85` as part of the map; its
  passing notices wait for the notice rule (L-342).
- One tip: `panel` .97, `keyEdge` edge (`warning` when it warns), padding 4,
  white text; the calculator's key tip turns white. The locked-trade tip
  keeps the game's tooltip look (vanilla). The calculator's passing note is
  a notice, left for the notice rule.
