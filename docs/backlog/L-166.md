# L-166 Villager trade helpers: favourites and assisted trading

Kind: Design, then Research for optional part 2
Status: Part 1 (Shift + right-click repeated trading) shipped in 0.2.1; part 2 is candidates waiting for the maintainer, not approved to build

Make a familiar villager's useful trades one action instead of finding and
repeating rows on every visit, through small extensions to the vanilla trade
screen rather than a replacement screen or more one-off toggles. Requested
by the maintainer 2026-10-14.

## Decided
- Keep part 1's Shift + right-click gesture and ordinary left/plain right
  click unchanged.
- Automation stays on the current trade screen: Lamium never scans for,
  walks to or opens other villagers. Keep the vanilla-looking UI.
- Keep L-168 Trade finder separate (it rerolls a profession to find an
  offer; L-166 trades once the offer exists). Share offer matching if
  practical, never workstation or profession automation.

## Open
Every stage is a candidate, not permission to build:
1. **Favourite offers.** A marker on a trade row, maybe middle-click
   (Shift + middle-click for shared). Check input mappings, other mods and
   vanilla pick-block first. Do not reorder vanilla rows at first. Scope:
   per-villager plus profession-wide defaults (individual wins), maybe an
   all-professions scope. Persist per world/server like other world-specific
   settings. Needs a stable villager identity across sessions/servers, never
   the transient actor runtime ID. Match offers by verified contents
   (enchantments, trade tier), not a row index; handle duplicates and
   dynamic prices.
2. **Trade all favourites on demand.** A separate unbound action processes
   the current villager's matched, unlocked favourites in a fixed order as
   far as stock, materials and output space allow, through part 1's vanilla
   trade path with per-request authoritative confirmation. Never fall back
   to an unfavourited row.
3. **Optional automatic run and close** (two options, both default off):
   run the queue when the screen opens; close the screen only after a
   normally completed run (at least one confirmed trade, no actionable
   favourite left, nothing pending). Any abnormal stop leaves it open.
   Never open another villager or drop items to continue.
4. **Faster batching (Research).** Vanilla result-slot Shift + click can
   craft several trades in one request, limited to what is already in the
   input slots (no refill; trace in TRADE-HELPERS.md). Explore verified
   refill -> vanilla multi-craft -> acknowledge -> revalidate; benchmark
   against the shipped path on local worlds and BDS first. Never synthesize
   trade packets or send more while a response is pending.
5. **Display.** Remaining uses, actionable count, minimal progress; keep
   favourites readable without hiding locked offers and their tooltips.
   Count limits and price caps are candidates only.

Questions for the maintainer: favourite scope and identity, gestures, queue
order, automatic run/close UX and success criterion, whether counts and caps
are worth the UI, whether batching justifies its compatibility surface.

## Next
Wait for the maintainer's choices. Any build must reconfirm villager, offer,
costs, lock/stock, inventory space and request result at every step; stop on
failed/stale responses, price or recipe change, no space, screen/focus/world/
dimension change, setting off or player input; never drop or overflow output.
Check local and BDS: two-input enchanted books, restocks/level-ups,
discounted prices, identical-looking offers, sold-out rows, no materials,
full inventory, interrupted close, network delay/loss, rejoin. Without a
stable identity or reliable input hook, fall back to a smaller manual mode.
