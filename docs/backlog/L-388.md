# L-388 One craft run for making a plan's steps and Mass Craft

Kind: Refactor (strong model)
Status: Three runs on one planner, seen in a local world (2026-11-23); whether Mass Craft joins it is open

`RecipePlace.cpp` holds four runs that drive the crafting screen frame by
frame: laying a recipe in once the grid empties (`going`), making a plan's
steps (`making`), crafting onto the pointer (`onto`) and Mass Craft
(`massCraft::run`). They repeated the same moves (wait for the grid to
empty and clear it, take the output as a Shift click does, drop the run
when its screen goes, log the end) with separate counters and patience.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-22)
- The recipe code is to be made simpler where that is right; this one
  waited, because the runs were tuned in game on timings the tests cannot
  reproduce, until L-385's server check (done on a local BDS, 2026-11-23).

## Known
Each run keeps the patience and counting it was tuned with; only the
shape is shared, and log lines stay word for word. `CraftRun.h`:
`craftRun::step` reads what the screen shows (`Seen`) and answers one
action; `rulesFor` keeps each run's numbers; tests in
`tests/CraftRunTests.cpp` follow each run's old behaviour. `going`,
`making` and `onto` are driven by it.

## Open
Mass Craft shares only the take and the grid's evacuation with the
others. Its throws, the pointer-held path (Hand), the room and surplus
drops and the 16-call step budget would become about ten actions whose
outcomes go back to the planner. Read from the code, not tried: that
looks like a rewrite of its most tuned part rather than a simplification.
Leave it, route only its lay and take through the planner, or convert it
whole?
