# L-287 Make the minimap arrows legible at every map size

Kind: Design
Status: Parked (2026-10-31), no release target; reopen with a new marker idea or if the Map Size 18 % distortion matters

The player and camera arrows (L-286) are drawn into the 256 px map texture.
On main (`b6ad8327`) the camera arrow draws well and most Map Size and Range
settings look clean, but around Map Size 18 % both arrows come out
distorted: the texture, arrow included, is minified to the map's on-screen
size and those scales alias the arrow's thin outline and notch. At larger
sizes the plain triangle does not read well as an arrow. The maintainer gave
up on the current look for now.

## Decided
- Do not re-tune the current glyph without a new idea.
- A replacement must read at the smallest Map Size without dominating it,
  keep the camera arrow distinguishable, and look like the map grew out of
  the game's own art.

## Open
A different marker rather than another glyph tweak, e.g. a direction wedge
on the map frame, the mockup's green player arrow, or the arrow drawn in the
HUD layer above the map.

## Tried
On branch `l286-minimap-camera-arrow` (kept locally, `b2f563e4` to
`ec66e53f`, not merged), each checked in game and rejected: small sizes
stayed hard to tell from the white radar dots; large ones showed a thin
edge, an obvious triangle or a grey mosaic.
- Screen-space arrow images (no minification with the map).
- Scaling with Map Size, 6 px floor.
- A two-unit outline; a notch-less triangle for small arrows.
- A head-and-shaft glyph.
- Hard black/white edges instead of anti-aliasing.
