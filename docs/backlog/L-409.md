# L-409 The item list leaves an empty dark strip at the screen's edge

Kind: Ready (Bug)
Status: Built, not checked in game

Beside the inventory the item list's panel on the right ended in a strip
with the dark ground and no items, at the screen's right edge (maintainer
2026-11-26).

## Decided
- The cells stand against the screen's edge and the width that holds no
  whole cell is next to the game's window, as the left region's panels
  have it (maintainer 2026-11-26).
- The favorites' cells follow the same rule where their panel ends at the
  right edge; their scroll bar keeps its place at the edge.
- The height left under the last row stays as it is.

## Known
- The right region runs from the game's window to the screen's edge
  (`band::regionsAround`, `BandLayout.h`) and the panel's ground fills it.
- A panel that ends at the screen's right edge moves its cells right by the
  width left over (`ItemListLayout::shift`, `SideLayout::shift`,
  `ItemListLayout.h`; tests in `tests/ItemListInputTests.cpp`). At 640
  units across with the 176 wide inventory that was about half a cell.

## Next
The maintainer looks at the inventory: the list's last column touches the
screen's edge, presses land on the cells, the favorites likewise when
their panel is on the right.
