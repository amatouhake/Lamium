# L-409 The item list leaves an empty dark strip at the screen's edge

Kind: Design (Bug)
Status: Open; the cause read in the code, the strip's place to be chosen (maintainer 2026-11-26)

Beside the inventory the item list's panel on the right ends in a strip
with the dark ground and no items, at the screen's right edge (maintainer
2026-11-26).

## Open
Where the width that is left goes:
- A: the cells stand against the screen's edge and the strip is next to the
  game's window, as the left region's panels already have it (mirrored).
- B: the cells stand in the middle, half the strip each side.
- C: the ground ends with the cells and the world shows at the edge.
- The same for the height left under the last row, and for the favorites'
  cells, or the width only?

## Known
- The right region runs from the game's window to the screen's edge
  (`band::regionsAround`, `BandLayout.h`) and the panel's ground fills it
  ("a dimmed panel to the screen's edge", `ItemList.cpp`).
- The cells start at the panel's left plus 2 and are 18 wide; as many
  columns as fit (`itemListIn`, `ItemListLayout.h`). What is left, up to a
  cell less a unit, plus the 2 of padding, is the strip. At 640 units
  across with the 176 wide inventory it is about half a cell.
- `SideLayout` (the favorites) places its cells the same way; on the left
  its strip is next to the window.
- Not seen in game by the one who read the code.
