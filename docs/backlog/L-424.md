# L-424 Schematic ghost look settings: size, opacity, outline width

Kind: Ready (strong model)
Status: Checked in game (`95133e32`); the three follow-ups built, in-game check next (2026-11-28)

The look settings agreed in
[L-421 Schematic ghost look and aiming by what is drawn](L-421.md), each
changed on its own. Contract: SCHEMATIC.md "Ghosts" and its settings line.

## Decided
- A Ghost look heading in the Schematics category: tint (as before), size,
  opacity, outline width, outline distance. Mistake marks stay under
  Check. Defaults keep the look before this item.
- Size: a slider 50-100% in 10% steps (100). The block model, block-entity
  blocks (chests, signs) and the outline shrink about the cell's centre and
  keep every face; liquids stay whole. A shrunk ghost is aimed at only
  through its shrunk cell box (target card, ghost pick, placement assist),
  so the ray passes between ghosts to inner ones and to real blocks.
  Mistake marks beside a shrunk ghost keep their faces.
- A bed, which the game draws as one model from its head, shrinks as one
  about its middle (maintainer 2026-11-28); its outline and each half's
  aim box follow the drawn bed, so the halves meet without a gap. Halves
  shrunk apart would need the model cut, which the renderer cannot.
- Opacity: 10-100% in 10% steps (100), experimental and not recommended in
  its help text; below 100 ghost faces go into the blended mesh. Left out
  of any later saved looks (L-421).
- Outline width: a slider 0-5 read as None, 1 px, 1/64 ... 4/64; 1 is game
  lines, 2 and up are bars. Saved under `ghostLineWidth`; the former
  `ghostLines` choice maps thin 1, normal 2, thick 3, none 0.
- The L-421 look trial (`ghost_look_trial`, F8) is removed; the settings
  cover its looks except outlines only.
