# L-284 Custom crosshair

Kind: Design
Status: Open, nothing decided; low priority, no release target

Let the player change the crosshair's appearance instead of keeping the
vanilla one. Requested on the Discord server (maintainer's recollection
2026-10-30; requester and date not recorded).

## Open
- What is customizable within the UI rules (rectangles and text only):
  style/shape, size, color from the palette.
- A Lamium-drawn crosshair needs the vanilla one hidden and must follow its
  contexts (hidden with Hide HUD, the states where vanilla changes or drops
  it).
- Whether it shares a settings home with [L-283](L-283.md) (both are about
  reading the aim point).
- Priority stays low as pure appearance unless the design ties it to
  visibility.

## Known
Read 2026-11-17 (SDK 26.60.4 headers; nothing tried in game):
- The vanilla crosshair is drawn by `HudCursorRenderer`, a
  `UICustomRenderer` whose `render(context, client, owner, int)` is virtual
  (members `mCrosshairMat`, `mCrosshair` texture): a vtable hook could skip
  it per frame, the way Lamium's HUD hides draw per draw (DESIGN: draw-only
  changes are decided per draw). `SceneFactory::createHudCrosshairOverlayScreen`
  is `MCNAPI`.
- Lamium draws its HUD once per frame on the HUD view (`SettingsScreen.cpp`
  `render`, `hudView`), so a Lamium crosshair would be drawn there and
  follows Hide HUD (`getHideHud`) as the other elements do.
- Which game states replace or hide the crosshair, and whether they go
  through `HudCursorRenderer`, is not known.
