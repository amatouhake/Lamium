# L-284 Custom crosshair

Kind: Design
Status: Open, nothing decided; low priority, no release target

Let the player change the crosshair's appearance instead of keeping the
vanilla one. Requested on the Discord server (maintainer's recollection
2026-10-30; requester and date not recorded).

## Open
- What is customizable within the UI rules (rectangles and text only):
  style/shape, size, color from the palette.
- A Lamium-drawn crosshair needs the vanilla one hidden and must follow its
  contexts (hidden with Hide HUD, the states where vanilla changes or drops
  it).
- Whether it shares a settings home with [L-283](L-283.md) (both are about
  reading the aim point).
- Priority stays low as pure appearance unless the design ties it to
  visibility.
