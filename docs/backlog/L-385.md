# L-385 Make a plan's steps from its "to make" tab

Kind: Design
Status: Direction agreed (maintainer 2026-11-21); the controls wait on a mockup

"To make" lists the plan's steps with their station, what can be made now
and what is needed. Making them still means opening each recipe. The
plan knows the recipe (How to make), the members and the count, so a
step can be laid in, or made, from its row.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- Both laying in and making are offered; making goes through an explicit
  control, never a bare press on a row. The look is agreed from a mockup.
- Making more than one round uses Mass Craft's run (L-165), so that
  needs its server check before the release that carries this.
- A row makes its step, as much as is still needed; a control on the
  "can make now" heading makes every step there, top down, so a step's
  product feeds the next. Only steps of the open station (a 2x2 recipe
  also in the inventory); others cannot be pressed and their tip names
  the station.
- "Can make now" counts what is carried only. Steps carried items fall
  short of but the stores would cover stand under "make after taking
  out", between "made in part" and "short"; an input held only in a store
  has a small chest on its slot (red stays for what nobody holds), and
  its tip lists the stores and counts, as "to gather"'s star does.
- After making, the plan works itself out again: made steps go to held,
  the next rise into "can make now".

## Open
- The controls for laying in and making: two buttons, one with a
  modifier, joined buttons, or laying in first and making on a second
  press ([demos/plan-make.html](../demos/plan-make.html)).
