# L-389 How to make: one way to offer an item's and a group's choices

Kind: Design
Status: Open; the members' order is agreed, the shape is being framed (maintainer 2026-11-22)

How to make's lines open their choices in two shapes. An item's line
opens rows: "automatic" (the rule's recipe as faint slots, or the item
itself where the rule gathers it), "gather", then each recipe as its
slots. A group's line opens a grid of slots: "any" first, then each
member; a group the plan traces past its kind (L-386) also gets a
"gather" row above the grid (seen in game 2026-11-22).

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-22)
- A group's members stand in the item list's creative order (oak,
  spruce, birch, ..., bamboo, crimson, warped), in How to make, the
  group's tip and the wheel; today it is the game's tag order.
- First the problems, then the shape. One shape for items and groups is
  not a goal in itself: what reads best wins.
- The word stays "集める" (gather), as the "集める物" tab says it.
- Re-dyeing recipes (another color of the same thing) fold into one
  row that opens.
- A group's choices say only which member and how the group itself is
  got; a member picked has its own ways as its line under "レシピ", as
  any item (the mockup nested them, which was part of what read as
  involved).
- A group's choices (demos/howto-choices-3.html): one line each, words
  from names alone, "<kind>から作る" where it is traced and "<group>を集める"
  always (these replace today's "*" cell, whose meaning differed by
  group), then the members in a grid from the start, a press deciding
  one; no heading or hint words. The "*" stays on the lines' pictures.
- From the first direction mockup: asking a group's two questions apart
  read as too involved; "automatic" as a mark on a row blurred the
  policy (which may change) with choosing the recipe it uses now, and
  the row in use by the rule had no green frame.

## Open: the problems (maintainer and agent, 2026-11-22)
Groups:
- "Gather" is a row of its own, and only for traced groups.
- "Any" stands among the members, told apart only by being first, its
  "*" and the tip: it shows a member's picture (sand's "any" and its
  sand look the same).
- "Any" and "gather" are hard to tell apart: one list answers two
  questions, which members count (any or one) and whether the group is
  made or gathered; "any" means made for planks, gathered for sand.
Recipes:
- Many recipes make the list very long (a white harness: about 30 rows,
  nearly all re-dyeing another harness).
- Order: the table's; the recipe from leather, glass and wool is not on
  top, re-dyeing comes first.
- "Automatic" repeats a recipe that also has a row of its own further
  down, and "Automatic (now: gather)" sits beside "gather" with the same
  picture: the difference (follows the rule or fixed) does not show.
- A recipe is named by its first input only: two rows read "from black
  harness" alike (white dye or bone meal), and long names are cut.
- "Gather" is offered for every item, also ones the world never gives.
