# L-389 How to make: one way to offer an item's and a group's choices

Kind: Design
Status: Open (maintainer 2026-11-22: to talk over, with a mockup)

How to make's lines open their choices in two shapes. An item's line
opens rows: "automatic" (the rule's recipe as faint slots, or the item
itself where the rule gathers it), "gather", then each recipe as its
slots. A group's line opens a grid of slots: "any" first, then each
member; a group the plan traces past its kind (L-386) also gets a
"gather" row above the grid. So "gather" is a row for some groups, is
missing for the others (where "any" already gathers), and stands apart
from "any" and the members, which sit side by side (seen in game
2026-11-22).

## Open
- One shape for both (rows, slots in a grid, or a row of kinds of
  choice over the members), and where "gather", "automatic" and "any"
  stand in it.
- Whether a group "any" says what it is traced to (planks from logs),
  and whether every group offers "gather" (for one not traced, "any"
  gathers already).
- How "Automatic: gather" (leather, diamond, a loop's base) reads beside
  the player's own "gather".
