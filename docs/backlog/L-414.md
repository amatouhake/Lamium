# L-414 Redstone power level overlay

Kind: Research
Status: Open; first step is finding why the resource pack way stopped working

Show the power level (0-15) of redstone in the world, so a circuit can be
read without guessing from the dust's shade (GitHub issue #84).

## Decided
- Investigate first; nothing is built or designed until the research below
  says what the client can read (maintainer 2026-11-28).

## Open
- What the resource pack approach did, in which game version it worked and
  what change stopped it. Reported second hand: it worked in a pack before
  and the old pack no longer does (maintainer 2026-11-28). Learn the
  mechanism only; take nothing from the pack (PROVENANCE.md).
- Whether the client knows each block's power without the server: the
  wire's `redstone_signal` state, and what other parts (repeaters,
  comparators, torches, powered blocks) carry on the client.
- What to show and where (dust only or every part, the range around the
  player): a Design question once the reads are known.

## Next
Find the pack or its description and the game version where it broke; then
read the SDK headers for the wire block's signal state and check it in a
local world and on a server.

## Known
- The light overlay already draws digits on the floor with bounded per-frame
  reading (LIGHT-OVERLAY.md); a power overlay could reuse that drawing.
