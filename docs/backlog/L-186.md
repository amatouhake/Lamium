# L-186 Improve schematic ghost legibility and reconsider translucency

Kind: Design / Research (strong model).
Status: Partly done: tint slider and outline widths shipped and checked; translucent ghosts open (maintainer: after the release).

Distinguish ghost blocks from real blocks while keeping their native colors
readable. In the reported screenshot (user feedback 2026-10-14) a pink
concrete ghost overlapped a light-blue overlay so much that the pink, and
even whether it was a ghost, became unclear.

## Decided
- Ghost tint is a 0-100% slider in 5% steps (`ghostTintPercent`; the earlier
  `ghostTint` choice maps to 100/40/0). Outlines are thin (one pixel), normal
  or thick; normal and thick are solid bars. Defaults (maintainer): 70% tint
  and Normal outlines, a little rugged, but the block and whether it is a
  ghost read best there.
- Where neighbors' bars meet, only the strongest result's copy is drawn
  within a section and its bar is slightly wider (step 0.002): wrong block >
  unknown block > wrong state > ghost/missing.
- Do not change the existing defaults without the maintainer's decision.

## Open
- Translucent ghosts as an optional mode, compared against the current
  outline/tint, line density, face shading, overlap/occlusion and depth
  behavior, not presumed better (dense transparent layers may add clutter).
  Evaluate dense redstone builds, ghost-on-ghost overlap, real/ghost
  boundaries, opaque/cut-out/blended/water blocks, ordinary and Vibrant
  Visuals graphics. Mode or opacity controls only after the comparison.
