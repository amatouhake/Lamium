# L-361 Brewing in the recipe view

Kind: Research
Status: Open, not started

A potion has no recipe in the recipe view: the client's recipe table, which
[L-359](L-359.md) reads, does not hold brewing (seen in game 2026-11-18).

## Known
- The table read today is `Recipes::mRecipes` (GAME-UPDATES.md); potion
  mixes are kept in `PotionBrewing`'s registries, whose `Mix<T>` the SDK
  gives no layout.
- Found without a layout (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-21): ask
  `isPotionIngredient` of every item, then `mix(ingredient, potion)` for
  each potion kind by aux: 210 mixes in a local world, all as the game
  brews them. Gunpowder and dragon's breath (the container mixes) need
  asking apart. About 110 ms at once: to be spread over frames.
- The list already tells potions apart by aux, so a recipe can name them.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-21)
- The face follows the brewing stand's screen, so a recipe maps onto it:
  the fuel at the top left, the ingredient at the top in the middle, the
  bottle before at the left, the middle bottle empty, the bottle after at
  the right ([demos/brewing-view.html](../demos/brewing-view.html), D).
- The fuel slot shows blaze powder: the stand takes nothing else.
- A plan counts by batches: one ingredient brews up to three bottles.
  Blaze powder is not counted: it would mostly be gathered for nothing.
- The face looks like the stand as far as it can, so it maps onto the
  real one (its tubes, coil, gauge; mockup v3).
