# L-373 Recipes, Plan and Stores as three features

Kind: Design, then Refactor
Status: Open; the split is agreed (maintainer 2026-11-20), its details are not

The Recipes setting holds three parts of different kinds: the recipe
viewer, the plan and the counted places ("stores"). The stores also serve
the Schematic HUD and the Materials tab, yet count only while Recipes and
its Plan are both on; the switch that counts what is carried sits only on
the plan window's Stores tab and is kept per world.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20)
- Three features, each working alone:
  - **Recipes**: the item list, the recipe view, the search, R, U and
    back; Favourites (and A) a part of it.
  - **Plan**: wanted, to gather, to make, W, the schematic's hand-over.
  - **Stores**: the counted places, their outlines in the world, whether
    what is carried counts; used by the Plan and by Schematics.
- This works because the bands are pages ([L-371](L-371.md)): with
  Recipes off the Plan's pages can still be placed, and the other way.
- Mass Craft stays a feature of its own.
- Every switch of the three has a row in the settings screen; a switch
  that also sits in a window (the Stores tab's) is the same saved value.

## Open (the agent's proposals, to confirm)
- "Count what is carried" once for all worlds instead of per world (it is
  a way of playing more than a world's fact); the per-world values would
  be dropped, the first one read kept.
- The way into the band layout editor: a "Layout" link row on each
  feature that has pages (Recipes, Favourites, Plan), opening the editor
  with that page selected, as the HUD features' "Placement and look"
  links do; inside a world it opens over the inventory, outside it is
  disabled.
- The section: side by side in Inventory for now; a section of their own
  is [L-340](L-340.md)'s question.
- Splitting RECIPES.md into the viewer's document and the plan's
  (with the stores), as the settings are split.

## Before building
Moving `recipesFavourites`, `recipesPlan` and `recipesStoreMarks` changes
the settings format (AGENTS.md: stop and ask). Old keys are read and carried
over, nothing is lost; the maintainer confirms before the change lands.
