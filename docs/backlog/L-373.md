# L-373 Recipes, Plan and Stores as three features

Kind: Design, then Refactor
Status: Agreed (maintainer 2026-11-20); not built

The Recipes setting holds three parts of different kinds: the recipe
viewer, the plan and the counted places ("stores"). The stores also serve
the Schematic HUD and the Materials tab, yet count only while Recipes and
its Plan are both on; what is carried counts by a switch on the plan
window's Stores tab.

## Decided (maintainer 2026-11-20)
- Three features, each working alone:
  - **Recipes**: the item list, the recipe view, the search, R, U and
    back; Favourites (and A) a part of it.
  - **Plan**: wanted, to gather, to make, W, the schematic's hand-over.
  - **Stores**: the counted places, their outlines in the world, whether
    what is carried counts; used by the Plan and by Schematics.
- This works because the bands are pages ([L-371](L-371.md)): with
  Recipes off the Plan's pages can still be placed, and the other way.
- Mass Craft stays a feature of its own.
- Every setting of the three has a row in the settings screen. What is
  carried is one place among the others, not a setting: like the ender
  chest and each store it has its switch in the stores list, kept with
  the world's places (maintainer: one kind of thing, simpler to read).
- The way into the band layout editor: a "Layout" link row on each
  feature that has pages (Recipes, Favourites, Plan), opening the editor
  with that page selected, as the HUD features' "Placement and look"
  links do; inside a world it opens over the inventory, outside it is
  disabled.
- RECIPES.md is split into the viewer's document and the plan's (with
  the stores), as the settings are.
- The rows stand side by side in Inventory for now; whether they get a
  section of their own is [L-340](L-340.md)'s question.
- The window opened from the bands stays one window, its tabs by feature,
  each shown while its feature is on: wanted, to gather and to make
  (Plan), How to make (Recipes), Stores (Stores). The settings screen
  holds how features behave, the window the player's data (wanted items,
  choices, places and their switches). A switch sits in both only where
  it changes what that tab shows (the schematic's hand-over on the
  wanted tab), as the same saved value.

## Before building
No release has had these settings (0.7.0 has no Recipes at all), so
`recipesFavourites`, `recipesPlan` and `recipesStoreMarks` move without
reading the old keys (maintainer 2026-11-20); a development install
takes the new defaults.
