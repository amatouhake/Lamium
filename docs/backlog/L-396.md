# L-396 Target Info settings: one order and one way of naming the rows

Kind: Ready
Status: Built, not checked in game (2026-11-23)

The Target Info rows went back and forth between what the card targets
and what it shows, the display rows were named two ways ("Show
identifier" beside "Icon"), "Other details" no longer said what it holds
after block entity data got its own switch (L-395), and HUD layout's
Contents listed some display rows but not Position and Distance.

## Decided (maintainer, 2026-11-23)
- Order: what the card targets first (Range, Up to the render distance,
  Show schematic information, Target water and lava), then what it shows
  in the card's order: Icon, Identifier, Health, Armor, Growth, States and
  details, Block entity data, Position, Distance (`Options.h`).
- A row that shows a part is named by the part alone: Identifier,
  Position, Distance ("ID", "位置", "距離"). Show schematic information
  keeps its name: it chooses the target.
- "Other details" is "States and details" ("状態と詳細"); the name may
  still change, the saved field (`targetStates`) does not.
- HUD layout's Contents for the target: Icon, Identifier, States and
  details, Block entity data, Position, Distance.
- Health, armor and growth keep no Off (not asked for).

## Next
Check in game: the settings order and names in English and Japanese; HUD
layout's Contents shows Position and Distance and their switches work.
