# L-371 Recipe screens: groundwork before the next features

Kind: Design
Status: Agreed (maintainer 2026-11-20); to be split into feature items

The recipe screens ([RECIPES.md](../RECIPES.md)) are to grow. The features
below are a direction only: each gets its own item, talk and mockups when
its turn comes; this item holds them and the groundwork they need.

## Decided (direction, maintainer 2026-11-20)
- To build: a search syntax (leave out a word, either of two words, a tag,
  the item's tooltip text); the slots of the open game window that match
  the search lit up; a key that puts the cursor in the search field; what
  a recipe gives back and how many to make so little is left over
  ([L-362](L-362.md)); each step's state in a breakdown (missing, can be
  made, partly, held).
- Ctrl + press on a listed item that can be made crafts one onto the
  pointer. A plain press and Shift keep placing (RECIPES.md "The item list").
- A recipe's own control chooses it as the item's recipe, there, without
  going to "How to make".
- A recipe's own control adds its item to "wanted" and opens its breakdown.
- Wanted, not now: a search history (how it would show is unclear); a list
  of recently viewed items (only once the bands' arrangement is settled).
- Unsure: favouriting a recipe rather than an item; nobody has needed it
  yet, its gain is to be thought out first.

## Decided: the bands' arrangement (maintainer 2026-11-20)
From three rounds of [demos/band-layout.html](../demos/band-layout.html):
- Regions beside, above and below the game's window, each split into
  panels along it (top to bottom beside, left to right above and below);
  no free positions. One page to a panel, its heading one line with the
  page's own switch and pager; tabs only if pages outgrow the room.
- Edited by dragging alone: a panel is grabbed anywhere; the layout after
  the drop shows while dragging; over another panel of its region it
  swaps, in another region it goes before or after the one it is over,
  into an empty region it fills it; a tray holds pages not placed. Lines
  between panels size them in 5 % steps. Over its own place nothing is
  said; a place that takes nothing says so. Reset layout (two presses)
  and Done (Esc), as in the HUD layout editor.
- Each panel keeps one row; a smaller screen shrinks panels in
  proportion and never folds them. A region too low for one row shows
  its panels at the foot of a side region (left for above, right for
  below) on that screen only, marked while editing.
- Nothing is dimmed where no panel lies.
- Taking a page out only hides it; its feature works by its setting.
- No button on the bands to start editing; the way in is settled with
  the settings.

## Open: groundwork
- Keys by context: moved to [L-372](L-372.md), Lamium-wide (maintainer
  2026-11-20). Presses on the recipe screens' parts stay fixed and listed.
- Settings: split into Recipes, Plan and Stores, in
  [L-373](L-373.md) (maintainer 2026-11-20).
- Stations. Agreed (maintainer 2026-11-20): the recipe view's tabs are
  kinds of work (crafting, cooking, stonecutting, smithing; later
  brewing and the like), each recipe once, with the icons of the
  stations that do it. Cooking takes in the furnace, blast furnace,
  smoker and both campfires: the game files one recipe under those
  stations' tags. This replaces "a recipe under each station that does
  it" (2026-11-19), whose point, where it can be made, the icons keep.
  A chosen recipe is kept by station and id today, so its notes need
  carrying over. A station's recipes (R, a left press) are how it is
  made; its uses (U, a right press) first what it does, only the recipes
  it can do ("cooking (blast furnace)"), then the recipes it goes into,
  none twice. A station's icon by a recipe opens its uses.
- The game's recipe book button: a setting, in [L-368](L-368.md).
- The item list's entries. Agreed (maintainer 2026-11-20): items no
  creative tab holds (water, lava) by a setting: always, or only where
  they can be taken out (creative, cheats; the default, with
  [L-360](L-360.md)), after the creative list; seeing them has its own
  worth. Each creative entry is kept with its data (fixes
  [L-363](L-363.md)'s tips). All variants are listed (every enchanted
  book's level, as the creative list has them); a fold is thought about
  only if that is a bother. The game's own creative groups
  (`CreativeGroupInfo` in the SDK) would be its natural basis.
