# L-371 Recipe screens: groundwork before the next features

Kind: Design
Status: Open; the direction is agreed (maintainer 2026-11-20), the groundwork is not yet discussed

The recipe screens ([RECIPES.md](../RECIPES.md)) are to grow a good deal.
The features chosen below are adopted in direction only: each still goes
through conversation and mockups, and most need groundwork first. This
item holds the direction and the groundwork; a feature gets its own item
when its turn comes.

## Decided (direction, maintainer 2026-11-20)
- To build: a search syntax (leave out a word, either of two words, a tag,
  the item's tooltip text); the slots of the open game window that match
  the search lit up; a key that puts the cursor in the search field; what
  a recipe gives back and how many to make so little is left over
  ([L-362](L-362.md)); each step's state in a breakdown (missing, can be
  made, partly, held).
- Ctrl + press on a listed item that can be made crafts one onto the
  pointer. A plain press and Shift keep placing (RECIPES.md "The item list").
- A recipe's own control chooses it as the item's recipe, there, without
  going to "How to make".
- A recipe's own control adds its item to "wanted" and opens its breakdown.
- Wanted, not now: a search history (how it would show is unclear); a list
  of recently viewed items (only once the bands' arrangement is settled).
- Unsure: favouriting a recipe rather than an item; nobody has needed it
  yet, its gain is to be thought out first.

## Open: groundwork
- The bands' arrangement ([demos/band-layout.html](../demos/band-layout.html)).
  Agreed (maintainer 2026-11-20): regions, panels split along a region and
  pages as tabs, not positions; regions above and below the game's window
  as well as beside it; every edit by dragging; a page joins tabs only
  where the player drops it, never because room is short; nothing is
  dimmed where no panel lies. Open: the second mockup's questions (a grip
  that moves a whole panel, each panel keeping one row, a region too low
  for one row, whether taking a page out only hides it).
- Keys by context: a conflict is judged across all actions in two contexts
  (gameplay, item screens). The recipe screens add more (the view open,
  the plan window open, the search field focused), and presses with Ctrl
  or Shift on Lamium's own parts, which are not bindings today.
- Settings: Recipes holds three parts (favourites, plan, store outlines);
  Mass Craft is apart; the schematic's hand-over switch sits on two
  screens; the plan's counted places also serve the Schematic HUD and fall
  back to what is carried when the plan is off ([L-340](L-340.md)).
- Stations: one tab per station today. Several stations doing the same
  work (the furnace and the blast furnace) may be one kind of recipe with
  its stations named; that is a layout question too.
- The game's recipe book button: whether it switches the item list between
  all items and what can be made.
- The item list's entries: items no recipe or creative tab offers (water,
  lava) listed, for taking out in creative ([L-360](L-360.md)).

## Next
Talk the groundwork through with the maintainer, starting with the bands'
arrangement, keys by context and settings.
