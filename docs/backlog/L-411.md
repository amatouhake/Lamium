# L-411 Radar faces that are hard to read

Kind: Design
Status: Open; proposals to be drawn outside the game from a dump of the models (`radar_icon_probe`)

After L-330 Radar: mob icons that draw wrongly, then icons by default, the
maintainer and a second look at the sweep sheet (2026-11-26) found faces
that do not read as their mob.

## Open
The maintainer's:
- Bogged: no face (moss and mushrooms over the skull).
- Camel, camel husk: the profile reads well, but an angled view was never
  compared; large.
- Creaking: small and hard to make out.
- Elder guardian: a mini-boss, larger than a guardian in the world, drawn
  the same size.
- Ghast, happy ghast: blurred, the eyes do not show; large.
- Goat, sniffer: eyes on the sides, an angled view never tried.
- Nautilus, zombie nautilus: only the shell; from the side the spiral and
  the eye would show.
- Panda: blurred.
- Phantom: just readable; rarely seen head-on in the world, and its wings
  are what tells it.
- Wolf: no pupils, and the nose reads as two pixels where the game has one.

From the sheet:
- Warden: a dark band 12 by 4; the tendrils beside the head make it 48
  units wide.
- Tropical fish: grey (its skin is tinted in the game) and jagged with its
  fins.
- Sulfur cube: no "head" part, stays a dot.
- Weaker: the dolphin's front has no feature; strider, silverfish,
  endermite and tadpole are small.

## Known
- Blur comes from halving by averaging (a face past 15 texels, or a skin
  finer than the model: the ghast's is 128 by 64 for 64 by 32 units).
- The wolf's snout stands on half units (`origin -1.5, 7.52`), where a
  pixel takes its texel from one side.
- The research build writes every renderer's parts, cubes, face rectangles
  and textures to `logs/radar-dump/` (`dumpModels`, `RadarFaces.cpp`), so
  proposals can be drawn without the game.

## Next
One world entry with the research build; then a sheet per kind with the
choices side by side (front, angled, side; with and without parts; halved
or whole) for the maintainer to pick from.
