# L-411 Radar faces that are hard to read

Kind: Design
Status: 14 kinds built from the maintainer's picks, not checked in game; the rest wait for new proposals

After L-330 Radar: mob icons that draw wrongly, then icons by default, the
maintainer and a second look at the sweep sheet (2026-11-26) found faces
that do not read as their mob.

## Decided
Picked from the sheet (maintainer 2026-11-26), in `faceRecipe`:
- Camel, camel husk: the profile, the head without its neck (10 by 8).
- Creaking: the trunk's two boxes, without the boards of branches.
- Elder guardian, warden: not halved, 16 a side (`wholeFaceSide`); the
  warden without its tendrils.
- Goat: from 45°. Nautilus, zombie nautilus: whole, from the side.
- Wolf, cat, ocelot, allay, vex, polar bear: the grid starts where the
  largest face does (`FaceShot::aligned`); the wolf's snout three wide
  (`oneSided`), its nose one texel.
- Tropical fish: stays as it is, fins and all; it reads as a fish.

## Open
Not taken on 2026-11-26; a second sheet shows new proposals, to pick from:
- Bogged: its greenish white eyes are in the layer drawn over its skin
  (`bogged_clothes`, the model's `overlay` geometry); proposed: that
  layer's head, with or without the mushrooms.
- Sulfur cube: the inner box's skin has no eyes; whether the outer layer
  (`sulfur_cube_outer`) has a face waits for the next dump. Until then the
  body, 16 a side or halved.
- Ghast, happy ghast: their box is stretched to 72 and 64 units, so the
  face was reduced by an uneven factor; proposed: at the skin's own 16 by
  16, even left and right, told apart by the mouth.
- Panda: aligned and without its ears it fits unhalved (13 by 10); with
  ears it is 17 wide. Strider: unhalved (16 by 14) it is even.
- Phantom: from 30° above with the wings' roots the eyes still show (17 by
  9); the head alone from above fits today's cell.
- Sniffer: from the front, aligned, its top 15 units unhalved.
- A face 17 wide needs a cell of 19 and a larger atlas.
- Aligning every kind's grid (some twenty faces change) is not decided.

Said, to build next: dolphin from 60°; guardian and squids halved by
picking a texel instead of averaging.

## Known
- Blur comes from halving by averaging (a face past 15 texels, or a skin
  finer than the model: the ghast's is 128 by 64 for 64 by 32 units).
- The research build writes every renderer's model and textures to
  `logs/radar-dump/` (`dumpModels`, `RadarFaces.cpp`). A port of the face
  building outside the game gives the game's pixels (VALIDATION-LOG
  2026-11-26), so proposals are drawn without the game. The textures are
  the game's: the dump and the sheets stay out of the repository.
- The nautilus' part is taken from the first geometry near the largest
  (`modelBoxes`): the armor's is 74 % of the default's, just under the
  75 % that would take it. A version move may tip it; the face would show
  the armor.
- The atlas cell is 18 texels for the whole faces: 196 cells, 179 used by
  every kind and look at once.

## Next
