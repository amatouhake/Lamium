# L-411 Radar faces that are hard to read

Kind: Design
Status: Proposals drawn and shown (2026-11-26); waiting for the maintainer's picks

After L-330 Radar: mob icons that draw wrongly, then icons by default, the
maintainer and a second look at the sweep sheet (2026-11-26) found faces
that do not read as their mob.

## Open
The maintainer's:
- Bogged: no face (moss and mushrooms over the skull).
- Camel, camel husk: the profile reads well, but an angled view was never
  compared; large.
- Creaking: small and hard to make out.
- Elder guardian: a mini-boss, larger than a guardian in the world, drawn
  the same size.
- Ghast, happy ghast: blurred, the eyes do not show; large.
- Goat, sniffer: eyes on the sides, an angled view never tried.
- Nautilus, zombie nautilus: only the shell; from the side the spiral and
  the eye would show.
- Panda: blurred.
- Phantom: just readable; rarely seen head-on in the world, and its wings
  are what tells it.
- Wolf: no pupils, and the nose reads as two pixels where the game has one.

From the sheet:
- Warden: a dark band 12 by 4; the tendrils beside the head make it 48
  units wide.
- Tropical fish: grey (its skin is tinted in the game) and jagged with its
  fins.
- Sulfur cube: no "head" part, stays a dot.
- Weaker: the dolphin's front has no feature; strider, silverfish,
  endermite and tadpole are small.

## Known
- Blur comes from halving by averaging (a face past 15 texels, or a skin
  finer than the model: the ghast's is 128 by 64 for 64 by 32 units).
- The wolf's head stands on half units upward (7.5 to 13.5): the picture's
  grid starts on whole units, so six rows of texels are spread over seven
  and the snout's top row lands on the eyes' (the pupils go); the snout is
  three wide on a head six wide, drawn four wide with its middle doubled
  (the nose reads as two). Cats, ocelots, allays and vexes have a doubled
  column the same way.
- The bogged's own skin has no dark eye sockets; its mushrooms and moss
  are what the face shows.
- The research build writes every renderer's parts, cubes, face rectangles
  and textures to `logs/radar-dump/` (`dumpModels`, `RadarFaces.cpp`). A
  port of the face building outside the game gives the same pixels as the
  game for all 178 faces (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-26), so proposals are
  drawn without the game. The textures are the game's: the dump and the
  sheets stay out of the repository.

## Next
The maintainer picks from the sheets (angles for goat, sniffer, camel and
dolphin; halving by picking a texel instead of averaging, or not halving;
parts left out for bogged, creaking, warden, tropical fish; the nautilus
and the phantom whole; the sulfur cube's body). Then `faceRecipe` and
`projectFace` take the picks, with the grid's start on the model's own
half units decided apart: it changes some twenty faces.
