# L-364 Draw "one of several" the agreed way on the other screens

Kind: Polish
Status: Built 2026-11-19, not seen in game

The look for choosing one of several was decided on 2026-11-19 (DESIGN
"Controls") and built in Recipes with `drawTab` and `tabRule`. Two other
places still draw it as buttons, Primary when chosen.

## Decided
- The Schematics screen's four tabs and the world map's dimensions take
  the dark look: a faint accent face over an accent bar.
- The Check tab's filters stay outlined pills; the world map's layers stay
  joined buttons.

## Open
- Whether the world map's bar, which packs its dimensions beside buttons,
  reads well with the rule under the strip, or wants the strip without it.
- Other places that choose one of several and were not listed.

## Next

under them, and the world map's dimensions, drawn without one.
