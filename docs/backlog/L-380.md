# L-380 Place a cooking recipe into the furnace

Kind: Research
Status: Open, not started

The recipe view's "place" lays a crafting recipe into the inventory's or
the crafting table's grid. The game's own recipe book places a cooking
recipe's input too, at the furnace, blast furnace and smoker (maintainer
2026-11-20, seen while checking [L-376](done/L-376.md)). With tabs per
process, the screen open names the tab whose recipes can be placed: the
furnace's screen smelting, the blast furnace's blasting, the smoker's
smoking.

## Open
- The furnace screens' collections for the input (and fuel) slots and the
  slot calls that move a stack there (Item transfer's and `craft_trace`'s
  findings for the crafting grid are the model).
- What a press lays in: one input, or a stack (Shift); whether the fuel
  slot is touched at all (the game's book does not choose fuel).
- Campfires have no screen: nothing to place there.

a short proposal.
