# L-116 Raw materials from the game's recipes

Kind: Research, then Design
Status: The client's recipe table read in game 2026-11-17 (local and an Endstone server, identical); pure calculation and recipe index built and tested (L-359); not wired to any screen

Show the raw materials needed for a schematic's missing blocks (logs for
planks, cobblestone for stone bricks) in the Materials tab, computed from
the recipes the client holds (`Level::getRecipes()`). Chosen 2026-10-08
(L-93 screen review). The same calculation feeds the plan across features
([L-359](L-359.md)).

## Decided
- Recipes come from the game, not an outside calculator (ResourceCalculator's
  data is GPL-3.0; see SCHEMATIC.md).
- The calculation is pure logic with tests:
  `src/features/recipes/RawMaterials.h` takes the wanted items and one
  chosen recipe per craftable item and returns raw materials, crafts per
  intermediate with spare output, and items left raw because their recipes
  lead back to themselves. Needs are summed across the list before rounding
  to whole crafts. `wanted` is everything still to be supplied; held stock
  is passed separately and taken off once per item, from the item's whole
  need. `tests/RawMaterialsTests.cpp`.

Maintainer 2026-11-17, on the agent's recommendation:
- Several recipes for one item: crafting table, then stonecutter, then
  furnace; within one, the lowest `mPriority`, then the recipe whose id is
  the result's name (oak planks from oak logs), then the id in alphabetical
  order. The player can switch per item.
- Items that convert into each other (nugget, ingot, block): the base is the
  form most recipes outside the group use; on a tie, the form the packing
  recipe uses several of. The others are made from the base; the base is
  traced only through recipes outside the group (smelting).
- The agent's widening of that rule, to confirm (2026-11-17): it holds for
  every loop of recipes, not only pairs. The real table has loops of three
  (copper ingot, block, waxed block), across stations (stone smelted from
  cobblestone, cobblestone cut from stone) and with a second ingredient
  (wool colors, honey). An item with a recipe from outside the loop is
  settled first; among equal recipes the one closest to the base wins
  (white wool and dye). With it no item's default plan loops (1045 items).
- Ingredient with several choices (any planks; raw iron or ore to smelt):
  one named group, held members added together. The trace goes past the
  group only after the player picks a member.
- Fuel is not counted; a furnace step shows the number of smelts. Raw totals
  are the main list, beside the steps with crafts and spare output. Held
  items are used first.

## Known
From the `recipe_trace` dumps (VALIDATION-LOG 2026-11-17, 1.26.60.05):
- `Recipes::mRecipes` is tag -> id -> `Recipe` (per-tag counts in the log).
  Skipped: the `deprecated` tag's 34 superseded variants and 15 special
  recipes without a result (maps, banners, fireworks, repair, armor trim).
  Results are loaded on the client.
- Smelting is in `mRecipes` too, one input per recipe; `mFurnaceResults`
  (tag hash -> `id << 16 | aux` -> result) only repeats it with tags spelled
  out, so the index does not need it.
- An ingredient is an item (aux 32767 means any) or an item tag; no other
  descriptor kind occurs. 15 tags are used (`planks` 13 members, `logs` 48,
  `coals`, `wooden_slabs`, `stone_crafting_materials`, ...);
  `forEachItemUntil` lists the members and stops when the callback returns
  true (its second argument is not a usable aux).
- A by-product is a further result (a cake's 3 buckets); the first result
  is the product.
- `mPriority`: an item's tag-based recipe carries the lowest value (-1 for
  sticks, torches, furnaces; variants 0 or 2), and the recipe named after
  its result is lower than the others in 94 of 97 unequal cases. Stonecutter
  and smelting recipes carry running numbers.
- 280 of 1011 crafting results have several recipes; 157 tie at the lowest
  priority (planks from log, stripped log or wood; dyed variants).
- 30 loops; the bases are the ingots, dusts and gems (smelted from ore),
  cobblestone, cobbled deepslate, wheat, slime ball, honey bottle (all
  gathered) and white wool (from string).
